QUICK-REF SYSTEM CHECKS (UNIVERSAL THRESHOLD = 14)
• The 14+ Rule: Any check, attack, or defense that totals 14 or higher after modifiers is a complete success. Anything under 14 is a failure.
• The Twin Die Engine: Spend 1 RP before rolling to gain Advantage (roll 2d20, keep high; max 1/turn). Hazards impose Disadvantage (roll 2d20, keep low). Advantage + Disadvantage cancel to a flat 1d20 roll.
• Action Phase (Attacking): Roll 1d20 + Weapon Attribute vs 14. Success deals calculated Flat Damage directly to target HP.

EASY PLAY D20 - SYSTEM CORE and ARMORY

One participant acts as the Guide; all others play Adventurers. Requires a single 20-sided die (d20) per player and these core rules.

1. HEROIC GENESIS (CHARACTER CREATION)

Building an Adventurer requires four foundational steps:

  • Background and Drive: Record a former trade and a core motivation.
  • Class Selection: Choose 1 Combat Archetype from the Playbook Codex. Your class dictates your battlefield role and determines which unique abilities you unlock as you level up.
  • Attributes: Base score of +1 in Might, Agility, and Will. Allocate +2 custom points across these (Max +4 per attribute).
  • Derived Vitals:
    • HP: (Might x 4) + 15
    • Movement: 2 + Agility (in Paces)
    • RP: Will x 2

2. THE ACTION ENGINE (FLAT 14)

All risky tasks, skill checks, attack rolls, and evasions target a static Universal Baseline Threshold of 14.

THE 14+ RULE: Any check, attack, or defense that totals 14 or higher after modifiers is a complete success. Anything under 14 is a failure.
  • Resolution: Roll 1d20 + Attribute Modifier. Sum of 14+ succeeds.
  • Rule of 20 and Criticals: Natural 20 on an attack is an Overdrive Strike (add Attribute Mod to flat damage twice). Natural 1 is a critical failure.
THE TWIN DIE ENGINE
Spend 1 RP before rolling to buy Advantage (2d20, keep high; max 1/turn). Severe hazards impose Disadvantage (2d20, keep low).
Cancellation Rule: Advantage + Disadvantage cancel out to a standard single 1d20 roll.

3. COMBAT STRUCTURE and TURN FLOW

Rounds represent 6 seconds of simultaneous action. System is 100% Player-Facing: no out-of-turn reactions.

  • Dynamic Guide Initiative: The Guide rolls 1d20 (+ Tier Mod) to set the encounter's Initiative Target. Players roll 1d20 + Agility vs this target. Players who meet or beat it act in the Fast Phase (before enemies); those who roll lower act in the Slow Phase (after enemies). Players win ties.
  • 1. Move Phase: Travel up to your Movement speed in paces (1 pace = 5 ft / 1 grid square).
  • 2. Action Phase: Attack, execute a class feature, drink a potion, or perform a maneuver. Roll 1d20 + Weapon Attr vs 14 to hit.
  • 3. Evasion Phase: When targeted on an enemy turn, roll 1d20 + Agility vs 14. 14+ = 0 damage. Fail = take flat enemy damage minus Armor DR.
DEATH and STABILIZATION (0 HP)
At 0 HP you collapse Prone and become Unconscious.
• Bleed Out: Permanently die in rounds equal to Will mod (min 1).
• Stabilization: Adjacent ally spends Action Phase for Will check vs 14 to revive you at 1 HP.
• Execution: Direct damage taken while at 0 HP causes instant permanent death.

Inventory Limit: Max equipped slots = 4 + Might modifier. Over-encumbrance imposes Disadvantage on all physical actions and halves your movement paces.

ACTION PHASE MANEUVERS (NON-ATTACK)
During your Action Phase, you can choose a tactical maneuver instead of executing an attack:
• Active Guard: Raise an equipped Shield to gain Advantage on all Evasion rolls until your next turn.
• Dash: Forfeit your attack to immediately move your total Movement paces a second time.
• Assist Ally: Grant an adjacent ally Advantage on their very next skill check or attack roll.
• Hide / Sneak: Roll Agility vs 14 to gain physical Concealment from enemy perception.
• Interact: Manipulate complex objects, pick a lock, pull a heavy lever, or hand a consumable item to a teammate.

UNIFIED PLAYBOOK CODEX (PART 1)

THE WARDEN

Narrative Role: The unmovable shield of the party. You secure physical space, hold the line, and endure what others cannot survive.

Recommended Loadout: Heavy Armor (DR -2), Shield (Active Guard), Light Melee (3 Dmg).

  • L1 Specialty: Vanguard Guard (Passive): End Move Phase adjacent to an ally to grant them Advantage on Evasion rolls while you remain conscious.
  • L1: Cleave (Heavy Weapon): After a hit, deal 2 flat damage to an adjacent secondary foe within 1 pace.
  • L2: Iron Stance [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP during Action Phase. Reduce all incoming flat damage by 2 points until your next turn (min 1).
  • L3: Brutal Force [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP on hit to add Might modifier to flat damage a second time.
  • L4: Steel Stance [2 RP]: Spend 2 RP during Action Phase. Reduce all incoming flat damage by 4 points until next turn (min 1).
  • L5: Unstoppable Defiance [2 RP]: When dropped to 0 HP, spend 2 RP to remain standing at 1 HP.
  • L6: Hardened Conditioning (Passive): Advantage on Might and Will checks vs environmental weather; rest in heavy armor without penalty.
  • L7: Sunder / Disarm [2 RP]: Spend 2 RP on hit to wrench target's weapon away OR permanently reduce target's Damage Reduction by 2.
  • L8: Brawl Mastery [4 RP]: Spend 4 RP on hit to grapple foe as physical cover (+Advantage on Evasion) OR pin a beast to remove its Action Phase.
Rule Clarification: Iron Stance stacks directly with your Armor DR. If you have Heavy Armor (DR -2) and activate Iron Stance (reduce 2 points), you subtract 4 total damage from every incoming hit.

THE BREAKER

Narrative Role: The aggressive entry specialist. You dictate battlefield positioning and punish enemies through sheer momentum.

Recommended Loadout: Heavy Melee (5 Dmg), Light Armor (DR -1), Improvised Tools.

  • L1 Specialty: Breacher (Passive): Never suffer movement penalties kicking open doors or smashing barricades.
  • L1: Drive Back [1 RP]: On Heavy Melee hit, shove target 1 pace back. Wall collision deals +2 crush damage.
  • L2: Adrenaline Surge [2 RP]: Double total movement paces for this turn.
  • L3: Concussive Blow [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP on hit. Target passes Might check vs 14 or becomes Dazed.
  • L4: Linebreaker [2 RP]: Move directly through enemy spaces, forcing them 1 pace aside (backed against wall = 2 crush damage).
  • L5: Relentless Assault [3 RP]: Spend 3 RP during Action Phase to make a second attack against a different adjacent target.
  • L6: Pack Mule (Passive): Maximum active inventory slots permanently increased by 2.
  • L7: Heave [2 RP]: Lift and throw heavy object up to 2 paces. Target passes Agility check vs 14 or takes 4 flat damage and falls Prone.
  • L8: Crash the Gates [4 RP]: Smash barrier on Move Phase to take immediate Action Phase with Advantage against unaware targets.
Rule Clarification: Crush Damage requires the enemy to be physically slammed into a heavy, unmoving barrier, such as a stone wall, a large vehicle, or a massive tree trunk.

THE PROWLER

Narrative Role: The scalpel. Bypassing security, flanking, and executing devastating close-quarters strikes.

Recommended Loadout: Twin Light Melee (3 Dmg +1 from Twin Fangs), Light Armor (DR -1), Ranged/Thrown Daggers.

  • L1 Specialty: Twin Fangs (Passive): Dual-wielding Light Melee adds +1 to flat damage output.
  • L1: Backstab [1 RP]: Positioned behind an engaged target, spend 1 RP for Advantage on attack and +2 flat damage.
  • L2: Prowler's Eye [1 RP]: Scan 3-pace area with Advantage vs 14 to spot hidden hazards and mechanisms.
  • L3: Nerve Strike [2 RP]: Spend 2 RP on hit. Target passes Might check vs 14 or becomes Pinned.
  • L4: Shadow Strike [2 RP]: Strike from concealment with Advantage; add Agility mod to flat damage a second time.
  • L5: Absolute Camouflage [3 RP]: In cover, enemies suffer Disadvantage on perception and attack rolls against you.
  • L6: Ghost Step (Passive): Advantage on silent movement; leave no physical footprints on natural terrain.
  • L7: Hamstring [2 RP]: Sever a tendon on hit; target movement permanently reduced to 1 pace until treated.
  • L8: Lethal Strike [4 RP]: Strike unaware non-boss target from behind to bypass armor and instantly drop them to 0 HP.
Rule Clarification: To attack from Concealment for Shadow Strike, you must be in dim light, behind full cover, or have successfully used your Action Phase to roll the Hide maneuver on a previous turn.

THE MARKSMAN

Narrative Role: Ranged overwatch. Securing angles, controlling enemy movement, and eliminating targets from afar.

Recommended Loadout: Ranged Weapon (2 Dmg), Light Armor (DR -1), Light Melee backup weapon.

  • L1 Specialty: Aimed Shot (Passive): Forfeit Move Phase to gain Advantage on your Ranged attack roll vs 14.
  • L1: Rig Chokepoint [1 RP]: Set tripwire in adjacent space; first enemy entering has movement reduced to 0 for that turn.
  • L2: Pinning Volley [2 RP]: Hail of fire at chokepoint; first enemy passing through automatically takes flat weapon damage.
  • L3: Crippling Shot [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP before attack. On hit, target's movement is halved on their next turn.
  • L4: Elevated Advantage [1 RP]: Firing from vertical elevation completely ignores target cover bonuses.
  • L5: Piercing Strike [3 RP]: Hit primary target with 2-Handed Ranged weapon and deal equal flat damage to secondary enemy directly behind.
  • L6: Eagle Eye (Passive): Effective range extended by +2 paces across all ranged weapon categories.
  • L7: Funneling Fire [2 RP]: Rapid burst forces target to immediately move 1 pace in a direction of your choice.
  • L8: Perfect Aim [4 RP]: Forfeit Move Phase; roll attack with Advantage, ignore cover, and add Will mod to flat damage.
Rule Clarification: To trigger Elevated Advantage, you must physically be positioned at least 1 full pace (5 feet) higher than your target, such as firing from a rooftop, a staircase, or a hovering vehicle.

THE SABOTEUR

Narrative Role: Environmental manipulator and demolitionist. Disabling gear and exploiting structural weaknesses.

Recommended Loadout: Light Melee (3 Dmg), Light Armor (DR -1), Ranged/Thrown explosives.

  • L1 Specialty: Saboteur (Passive): Automatically dismantle simple mechanical locks and traps given 1 full minute outside combat.
  • L1: Structural Weakness [1 RP]: Action Phase Will check vs 14; next successful attack against target by any ally ignores target's Damage Reduction.
  • L2: Blinding Debris [1 RP]: Throw ash/debris; Agility check vs 14 grants the target ally Advantage on their next Evasion roll against that enemy.
  • L3: Cripple Target [2 RP]: Action Phase check vs 14 forces target to grant Advantage on all player Evasion rolls against it until it forfeits an Action Phase to recover.
  • L4: Rig Trap [2 RP]: Rig environmental object; Agility check vs 14 drops object for 8 flat damage and knocks Prone.
  • L5: Master of Ruin [3 RP]: Exploit structural flaws in building/vehicle to cause localized physical collapse.
  • L6: Scavenger's Eye (Passive): Identify mechanical components to salvage usable tools from disabled technology.
  • L7: Noxious Irritant [2 RP]: Throw chemical vial at 1-pace spot; enemies starting turn in space grant Advantage on all player attacks and Evasion rolls.
  • L8: Volatile Reaction [4 RP]: Trigger explosive hazard dealing 10 flat damage and Dazed to everything within 2 paces.
Rule Clarification: Valid environmental objects for Rig Trap include heavy chandeliers, stacked cargo crates, loose rock formations, or unstable scaffolding directly above the target.

THE SURVIVALIST

Narrative Role: Field medic and tracker. Keeping squad alive through grueling conditions.

Recommended Loadout: Ranged/Thrown weapons (1 Dmg), Light Armor (DR -1), survival and medical gear.

  • L1 Specialty: Combat Medic (Passive): Stabilize dying ally within 1 pace using Move Phase (Will check vs 14).
  • L1: Drag to Safety [1 RP]: Grab unconscious or prone ally during Move Phase and drag them full movement paces.
  • L2: Aggressive Rouse [1 RP]: Action Phase instantly removes one physical condition (like Dazed) from an adjacent ally.
  • L3: Pressure Dressing [2 RP]: Bind wound; padding reduces incoming damage of very next attack hitting ally by 3 points.
  • L4: Forage [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP outside combat to automatically locate sustenance to sustain party for one day.
  • L5: Field Surgery [3 RP]: Short Breather procedure restores ally HP equal to twice your Will modifier.
  • L6: Iron Gut (Passive): Advantage vs natural toxins/diseases; require half standard daily rations.
  • L7: Potent Stimulant [2 RP]: Administer brew; ally regains 1 RP and +2 flat damage on next attack (takes 2 crash damage at combat end).
  • L8: Extreme Triage [4 RP]: Save dying ally from lethal hit; instantly wake at 1 HP with permanent maiming.

THE GUIDE'S MANUAL

1. ADJUDICATING THRESHOLDS and SOCIAL CHECKS

The Guide never rolls for task difficulties or monster attack hits. All player actions strictly target the static Universal Baseline Threshold of 14.

  • Grant Advantage: High ground, tactical surprise, or ideal tools in favorable contexts.
  • Impose Disadvantage: Heavy physical strain, slick ice, blindness, or heavy target concealment.
  • Social and Exploration: Call for a Will vs 14 check to persuade, deceive, or interrogate an NPC. Use Agility vs 14 for stealth, climbing, or acrobatics. Use Might vs 14 for lifting heavy rubble, breaking doors, or enduring extreme physical labor.
WHEN DOES THE GUIDE ROLL A D20?
To maintain 100% player agency, the Guide only rolls d20s for high-impact world events:
1. Initiative Target: Roll 1d20 (+ Tier Mod) at combat start to set Fast/Slow turn threshold.
2. Morale Checks: Roll 1d20 when an enemy squad loses 50% of its members or leader.
3. Boss Escalation: Roll 1d20 when a Boss hits 50% HP (1-10: Spawn 2 Minions, 11-20: Trigger Major Hazard).
4. Failing Forward: Roll 1d20 to decide narrative complication severity on failed player checks.

2. ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARD DAMAGE

Environmental dangers bypass armor DR unless hard cover is actively present:

  • Minor Hazard (5 Flat Damage): Minor toxins, barbed wire scrapes, sprained ankles, minor electrical sparks.
  • Major Hazard (10 Flat Damage): Deep laceration traps, kinetic deadfalls, acid baths, high-voltage shocks.
  • Lethal Hazard (15 Flat Damage): Fuel explosions, massive cave-ins, magma submersion, atmospheric depressurization.

Combat Traits and Evasion Rules:

  • Tier 1 and 2: Standard Evasion vs 14. Minions drop in 1 hit.
  • Tier 3 (Elite): Hard Target: Player attacks suffer Disadvantage unless striking from concealment. Tactical: Forces Disadvantage on player Evasion if target is Prone or Dazed.
  • Tier 4 (Boss): Legendary Defense: Player attacks naturally suffer Disadvantage. Frenzy: Bosses take 2 Actions/turn (players roll Evasion twice).

4. LEVEL SCALING and ENCOUNTER TEMPLATES

Level Scaling Brackets:

  • Veteran Party (L3-5): Enemies gain +5 HP and +1 Damage (Bosses +15 HP).
  • Apex Party (L6-8): Enemies gain +10 HP, +2 Damage, and +1 DR (Bosses +30 HP).

4-Player Encounter Templates and Loot Drops:

  • Light Skirmish: 4 Minions, 2 Standards. (Rewards 1 Minor Loot Tag).
  • Tactical Clash: 3 Minions, 2 Standards, 1 Elite. (Rewards 1 Major Loot Tag).
  • Heavy Assault: 4 Standards, 2 Elites. (Rewards 2 Major Loot Tags).
  • Apex Boss Fight: 5 Minions, 1 Boss. (Rewards 2 Major Loot Tags + 1 Enhancer).

Enemy Modifiers:

  • Heavy / Armored: Add -1 DR to enemy (halve their movement).
  • Agile / Skirmisher: Player Evasion suffers Disadvantage (+2 enemy movement, -2 enemy HP).
  • Lethal / Sniper: Add +2 incoming Flat Damage (-5 enemy HP).
  • Dynamic Morale: When losing half their squad or leader, the Guide rolls 1d20. Enemies rout, dropping Loot/Intel, if the roll matches these exact ranges: 10 through 20 (Minions Only), 13 through 20 (Standard Present), or 16 through 20 (Elite Present). Bosses never flee.

5. TACTICAL, VISIBILITY and REST RULES

  • Partial Cover: Crouching behind cover grants Advantage on Evasion vs Ranged attacks.
  • Full Cover: Cannot be targeted until enemies maneuver around the barrier.
  • Visibility: Dim Light/Fog = Disadvantage on Perception and Ranged attacks. Pitch Black = cannot target beyond 1 pace; Disadvantage on Evasion.
  • Rest & Recovery: Short Breather: HP = Might mod, RP = Will mod. Rough Sleep: HP & RP = sum of all attributes. Sanctuary Rest: Full restore.

THE GUIDE'S GOLDEN RULE: FAILING FORWARD

On a failed Out-of-Combat Check vs 14, avoid simply saying "nothing happens." Introduce a complication: picking a lock alerts a guard; deciphering a rune causes mental shock; repairing an engine burns out a rare component. Roll 1d20 to decide severity (1-10 = Minor complication like noise or delays, 11-20 = Major complication like tool breakage or immediately taking 5 Flat Damage). Every failed roll pushes the narrative forward with fresh tension.

APPENDIX: UNIVERSAL GENRE ADAPTATION

Easy Play D20 functions as a universal narrative chassis. Because all mechanics rely on static flat damage, uniform baseline thresholds, and abstract Loot Tags rather than complex currency math, the system adapts instantly to any setting.

1. WEALTH, LOOT and NARRATIVE ECONOMY

Wealth is tracked on the Character Sheet using Loot Tags rather than individual coins or credits. The Guide awards setting-appropriate Loot Tags after combat or exploration. Players trade these tags in settlements for Sanctuary Rests, gear, bribes, or local favors.

2. ADAPTING THE UNIVERSAL THRESHOLD

The baseline Threshold of 14 is mathematically tuned to provide a gritty, highly tactical combat experience where characters will miss attacks and fail dodges roughly half the time without spending RP. However, the Guide can universally adjust this number to fit the desired tone of the campaign:

  • Super-Heroic Action (Target 12): Lowers the barrier to entry, ensuring players succeed more often without spending RP. Perfect for fast-paced, pulp-action settings.
  • Survival Horror (Target 16): Drastically increases the failure rate, forcing players to burn RP constantly just to survive standard encounters. Best for grimdark or horror campaigns.

3. ADAPTING EQUIPMENT and ARMORY

Armory mechanics remain mathematically identical across all settings. Reskin item names during Character Creation to fit the narrative:

  • Light Armor (DR -1): Leather jerkin (Fantasy), Kevlar weave (Cyberpunk), tactical vest (Urban Fantasy), scrap vest (Post-Apoc).
  • Heavy Armor (DR -2): Full plate mail (Fantasy), Powered Exosuit (Sci-Fi), Steam-powered rig (Steampunk), combat shell (Cyberpunk).
  • Active Guard (Shield): Wooden tower shield (Fantasy), hard-light barrier (Sci-Fi), steel riot shield (Cyberpunk/Modern).
  • Light Melee (3 Flat Dmg): Shortsword (Fantasy), vibro-blade (Cyberpunk), wrench (Post-Apoc), hunting knife (Space Western).
  • Heavy Melee (5 Flat Dmg): Greataxe (Fantasy), kinetic hammer (Sci-Fi), sledgehammer (Steampunk), power sword (Grimdark).
  • Ranged Weapon (2 Flat Dmg): Longbow (Fantasy), plasma rifle (Sci-Fi), lever-action repeater (Space Western), sniper (Modern).

4. CONSUMABLE and UTILITY LOOT MECHANICS

Assign setting-flavored consumables to one of three standardized mechanical templates. Using any consumable during combat costs an Action Phase:

  • Restoratives (Potions, Hyper-Med Kits, Stim-Packs): Restores 5 HP, regains 1 RP, OR clears one physical condition (such as Dazed or Pinned).
  • Enhancers (Black Market Ammo, Experimental Serums, Plasma Charges): Grants +2 Flat Damage to your next hit, OR forces your next attack to ignore target Armor DR.
  • Utility and Survival (Warding Components, Scavenged Parts, Rations): Grants Advantage on a specific Out-of-Combat check, OR fulfills food/water requirements needed to safely complete a Rough Sleep or Sanctuary Rest without taking hazard damage.

5. MAGIC, PSIONICS and CYBERWARE

Magic and extraordinary powers are handled through narrative reskinning. A Wizard, Psion, or Cyborg utilizes the standard class features and weapon math dressed in setting flavor:

  • Spells as Weapons: A Wizard's "Ranged Weapon" is a Firebolt. A Psion's "Heavy Melee" is a telekinetic force-crush. A Cyborg's "Ranged/Thrown" weapon is a micro-missile.
  • Abilities as Magic: A Breaker's Concussive Blow can be narrated as a kinetic shockwave spell or hydraulic cyber-punch. A Saboteur's Structural Weakness can be a disintegrating magic hex or acid vial.

6. VEHICLES, MOUNTS and CHASES

When operating a vehicle or riding a mount (starfighter, horse, rover, hover-skiff), your character's movement capacity equals the vehicle's movement speed in paces. Vehicles grant their own Armor DR while occupied. When a vehicle reaches 0 HP, it is destroyed, forcing occupants to suffer a Lethal Hazard (15 Flat Damage). During chase sequences or hazardous piloting, the driver must pass an Agility Check vs 14 to maintain control; failure results in a crash, inflicting Major Environmental Hazard Damage (10 Flat Damage) to all passengers.
