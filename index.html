CHARACTER CREATION

The Three Pillars of Adventure

Might [Brawn / Force]: Heavy physical impact, breaking barriers, two-handed combat tracking, and scaling Health Points.

Agility [Reflexes / Speed]: Precise weapon combat accuracy, rapid stealth, tactical pacing, and your Defense Target.

Will [Mind / Perception]: Sensory scanning, long-range tracking, arcane hex manipulation, and maximum Resolute Points.

Step-by-Step

1. Select Class: Warrior, Scout, or Adept.

2. Distribute Attributes: Start with +1 in all. Spend 4 additional points.

3. Calculate Vitals: Compute HP, Defense, RP, and Movement based on your Attributes.

4. Equip Gear: Choose your starting armor and weaponry based on your class restrictions.

CORE RULES REFERENCE

1. How to Roll (The Action d20 Engine)

  • How Many Dice: Roll 1d20 + Attribute modifier. Spend 1 RP for Advantage to roll 2d20 (keep highest). Under Disadvantage, roll 2d20 and keep the lowest.
  • Why You Roll: To meet or exceed the static numerical Target Difficulty threshold set by the Guide.
  • How It Resolves: Check total against target score. Success means perfect execution. No reactions exist outside your active turn.

[!] The Twin Die Engine (Resource Resolution)

Spend 1 RP during your Action Phase to buy Advantage. Roll an explicit second d20 concurrently (2d20) and evaluate using the highest single face value.

If combat factors impose Disadvantage, you must roll 2d20 and resolve the action using the lowest face value.

GEAR RULES

Vitals & Static Equipment Integration

HEALTH POINTS (HP) = (Might Modifier × 3) + 10 | DEFENSE TARGET = 10 + Agility + Armor + Shield

RESOLUTE POINTS (RP) = Will Modifier × 2 | MOVEMENT = 2 paces + Agility (Climb/Swim: Half Speed)

  • Light / Medium / Heavy Armor: Grants a flat +1 / +2 / +3 bonus directly to your baseline static Defense Target.
  • Shields (The Passive Wall): Occupies 1 hand. Grants a flat +1 bonus to your static Defense Target. Restricts two-handed weaponry deployment.

Flat Weapon Damage Engine & Hand Constraints

Hand Limit Rule: Wielding a shield uses 1 hand, locking out heavy weapons, 2-handed ranged sniper stances, or playbook abilities like Cleave or Twin Fangs. No dice are rolled for damage. Hits deal Weapon Base + Attribute modifier directly to HP (minimum 1).

  • Ranged Weapons (Base 1 Damage): [All Classes] 1-Handed Layout = 3 paces range (allows shield choice). 2-Handed Layout = 10 paces range (prevents shield).
  • Light Melee Weapons (Base 3 Damage): [Warrior / Scout Only. Requires 1 hand] Fast close-quarters mobility tracking.
  • Heavy Melee Weapons (Base 5 Damage): [Warrior Only. Requires 2 hands] Pure crushing devastation. Excludes shield usage.
  • Basic Arcane Spells/Hexes (Base 4 Damage): [Adept Only. Requires 1 free hand] Kinetic bursts. Costs exactly 1 RP per cast.
  • Overdrive Strike: Natural 20 rolls add your matching Attribute Modifier to the execution a second time.

THE WARRIOR

L1 Specialty: Twin Fangs (Passive): When wielding two Light Melee weapons simultaneously, add +1 to your total flat damage output on all successful close-combat strikes. (Requires both hands occupied by weapons; incompatible with Shields).

L1: Cleave (Heavy Weapon Only): Immediately following a successful melee strike with a Heavy Weapon, deal your normal flat damage to the primary target, and inflict exactly 2 flat damage to one adjacent secondary foe within arm's reach. (Requires 2 hands; incompatible with Shields).

L2: Iron Hide [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP during the Damage Phase when you are hit by an attack. You instantly blunt the blow, reducing the incoming damage by 2 points (minimum 1 damage taken).

L3: Brutal Force [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP immediately after confirming a successful melee hit. You completely double your Might modifier's contribution to that attack's flat damage calculation.

L4: Steel Hide [2 RP]: Spend 2 RP during the Damage Phase when you are hit by an attack. You instantly blunt the blow, reducing the incoming damage by 4 points (minimum 1 damage taken).

L5: Unstoppable Defiance [2 RP]: Spend 2 RP when an attack would reduce your HP to 0. You completely negate the knockout blow, remaining active and standing at exactly 1 HP.

THE SCOUT

L1: Fade Step (Passive): During your Move Phase, you may freely disengage from active close combat and step directly behind an enemy without triggering any movement penalties or complications.

L1: Climber (Passive): Climbing costs standard movement paces rather than double, completely bypassing the standard half-speed constraint.

L1: Backstab [1 RP]: When you are positioned directly behind a target engaged by an active ally from the front, spend 1 RP to gain Advantage (roll 2d20, keep highest) on your attack roll and add +2 to your flat damage. (Requires a Light Melee weapon).

L2: Infiltrator's Eye [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP to automatically succeed without needing a Will check, instantly revealing all hidden traps, secret pathways, footprints, or ambushes in your immediate zone.

L3 Specialty: Poison Pulse [2 RP]: Spend 2 RP upon landing a successful weapon attack to Stun the target. The Stun status completely skips the target's next action phase and lasts until the start of that target's subsequent turn.

L4: Shadow Strike [2 RP]: Spend 2 RP during your Action Phase to strike from absolute concealment. The attack automatically hits without requiring a d20 roll, dealing your flat weapon base damage + your Agility modifier.

L5: Vanish [3 RP]: Spend 3 RP when targeted by an enemy attack. You instantly dissolve into environmental cover, breaking line of sight and causing the incoming attack to automatically fail.

THE ADEPT

L1: Shatter-Blast [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP to direct a kinetic shockwave at a visible target. On a successful Will check against their Defense Target, you inflict flat damage equal to your Will modifier + 6.

L1: Trickery (Passive): You may freely produce minor magical effects—such as a brief spark, a shadow flare, a faint whisper, or a slight draft—without requiring an execution roll or an RP spend.

L2: Pyrokinesis [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP to instantly channel molecular friction, automatically igniting candles, campfires, torches, or any highly combustible environmental item within your direct line of sight.

L3 Specialty: Aegis Shield [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP when an ally in your line of sight is targeted by an attack (before the d20 accuracy roll is made). You instantly grant them a +2 bonus to their Defense Target against that specific attack.

L4: Chthonic Hex [2 RP]: Spend 2 RP to curse a visible foe for the duration of the combat encounter. While active, anytime that target suffers flat damage from any source, they take an additional +1 flat damage.

L5: Runic Storm [3 RP]: Spend 3 RP to release a surge of raw kinetic energy, constructing a protective field extending 2 paces outward in all directions from your character. This field automatically blocks all incoming standard projectile attacks. Duration: Lasts until the start of your next turn.
