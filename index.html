EASY PLAY D20 — SYSTEM CORE (NO-TN EDITION)

1. THE UNIFIED THRESHOLD (FLAT 14)

Target Difficulties and enemy Defense Targets (DT) are completely removed[cite: 1]. Every risky action, skill attempt, attack roll, and evasion check targets a Universal Baseline Threshold of 14.

  • Standard Action: Roll 1d20 + Attribute Modifier vs 14.
  • Twin Die Engine: Spend 1 RP before rolling to buy Advantage (Roll 2d20, keep highest)[cite: 1].
  • Difficulty & Conditions: Favorable situations grant Advantage[cite: 1]. Heavy resistance or severe hazards impose Disadvantage (Roll 2d20, keep lowest)[cite: 1].

2. 100% PLAYER-FACING COMBAT

The Guide never rolls dice in combat[cite: 1]. Players make all attack and evasion rolls against 14.

  • Attacking (Action Phase): Roll 1d20 + Attribute Mod vs 14[cite: 1]. Hit = deal flat weapon damage directly to HP[cite: 1]. Natural 20 (Overdrive Strike) = add matching Attribute Mod a second time[cite: 1].
  • Evading (Enemy Turn): When targeted by an attack, roll 1d20 + Agility Mod vs 14. Success (14+) = dodge completely. Failure (<14) = take incoming flat damage.

3. DAMAGE REDUCTION (DR)

Armor subtracts incoming flat damage directly when you fail an Evasion Roll:

  • Light Armor: DR -1 (Subtracts 1 from incoming damage taken).
  • Heavy Armor: DR -2 (Requires 3 Might) (Subtracts 2 from incoming damage taken)[cite: 1].
  • Shield: Active Block — Once per round, gain Advantage on a single Evasion Roll[cite: 1].
