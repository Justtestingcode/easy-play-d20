QUICK-REF SYSTEM CHECKS (UNIVERSAL BASELINE THRESHOLD = 14)
• The Twin Die Engine: Spend 1 Resolute Point (RP) before rolling to gain Advantage (roll 2d20 and take the higher value). A maximum of 1 RP may be spent to buy Advantage per turn. Environmental hazards or severe obstacles impose Disadvantage (roll 2d20 and keep the lower value). If Advantage and Disadvantage apply simultaneously, they cancel each other out completely, resulting in a standard single 1d20 roll.
• Out-of-Combat Check: Roll 1d20 + the appropriate Attribute Modifier against the Baseline Threshold of 14. A final total of 14 or higher indicates success.
• Action Phase (Attacking): Roll 1d20 + the designated Weapon Attribute Modifier vs 14. If the roll succeeds (14+), you instantly inflict your calculated Total Flat Damage directly to the enemy's Health Points.
• Evasion Phase (Defending): On an enemy's turn, when targeted by an attack, roll 1d20 + Agility Modifier vs 14. Achieving a 14 or higher completely dodges the blow for 0 damage. Failing (<14) means taking incoming flat enemy damage minus your total Armor DR.
• Dying & Bleed Out (0 HP): Upon hitting 0 HP, you become Unconscious. You have a bleed-out timer equal to your Will modifier in rounds (minimum 1 round) before death becomes permanent. An adjacent ally must spend an Action Phase to pass a Will Check vs 14 to stabilize you.

EASY PLAY D20 — SYSTEM CORE & ARMORY

One participant assumes the narrative role of the Guide, while all other participants play Adventurers. To play, you require a single 20-sided die (d20) and these core rules.

1. HEROIC GENESIS (CHARACTER CREATION)

Building an Adventurer is structured around four simple foundational steps:

  • Background & Drive: Record a former trade or profession alongside a singular personal Drive that motivates your adventurer.
  • Class Selection: Select one of the six specialized Combat Archetypes detailed in the Unified Playbook Codex.
  • Attribute Allocation: All characters begin with a baseline score of +1 in Might, Agility, and Will. You receive 2 additional custom points to allocate across these attributes. Individual attribute modifiers cannot exceed +4.
  • Derived Vitals: Calculate your derived vital statistics directly onto your Character Sheet:
    • Health Points (HP): (Might Modifier × 4) + 15
    • Movement Speed: 2 + Agility Modifier (in Paces)
    • Resolute Points (RP): Will Modifier × 2

2. THE ACTION ENGINE (UNIVERSAL THRESHOLD 14)

Every non-trivial task, physical effort, attack roll, and defensive evasion check targets a single static target number known as the Universal Baseline Threshold of 14.

  • How Checks are Resolved: Roll 1d20 and add the relevant Attribute Modifier. If the final sum equals or exceeds 14, the attempt succeeds.
  • The Rule of 20 & Critical Hits: Rolling a Natural 20 on an attack roll triggers an Overdrive Strike, allowing you to add your Attribute Modifier to flat damage a second time. Outside of combat, a Natural 20 represents an exceptional, dramatic success. A Natural 1 is always an unmitigated critical failure.
THE TWIN DIE ENGINE (ADVANTAGE & DISADVANTAGE)

Whenever you desire extra certainty, you may spend 1 Resolute Point (RP) prior to rolling to gain Advantage (roll 2d20 and keep the higher outcome). Players are strictly limited to purchasing Advantage once per turn.

Environmental dangers, tactical handicaps, or severe physical impairments force you to roll with Disadvantage (roll 2d20 and keep the lower outcome).

The Cancellation Rule: If you are subject to Disadvantage but spend 1 RP to buy Advantage, both effects cancel each other out completely. You make a single standard 1d20 roll without taking the higher or lower die.

3. COMBAT STRUCTURE & TURN FLOW

Combat is split into a structured series of rounds, with each round representing 6 seconds of real-time battlefield action. Easy Play D20 utilizes a 100% Player-Facing system: there are no reactions taken outside of your turn, and the Guide never rolls attack dice.

  • Initiative Phase: At the start of combat, every player rolls 1d20 + Agility Modifier vs 14. Characters who meet or exceed 14 act in the Player Turn Phase before enemy forces; those who fail act after the enemy forces. Players win all ties.
  • 1. Move Phase: During the Move Phase, a character may travel a distance up to their total Movement attribute in paces (1 pace = ~5 feet). For example, an Adventurer with an Agility of +3 has a Movement capacity of 5 paces, allowing them to cover up to 25 feet or 5 standard grid squares.
  • 2. Action Phase: Spend your Action Phase to execute an attack, deploy a class ability, utilize a consumable item, or brace behind cover. Attack rolls require rolling 1d20 + Weapon Attribute Modifier vs 14. A success deals your full calculated Total Flat Damage directly to the target's HP pool.
  • 3. Evasion Phase (Enemy Turn): When an enemy targets your character on their turn, you roll an active Evasion Check consisting of 1d20 + Agility Modifier vs 14. Rolling 14 or higher results in a complete dodge, parry, or block, causing you to take 0 damage. A total below 14 means the blow connects, and you suffer the enemy's flat incoming damage minus your active Armor DR.
DEATH, BLEED OUT & STABILIZATION (0 HP)

When reduced to 0 HP, your character collapses Prone, drops all held items, and becomes Unconscious. While Unconscious, you cannot take Move or Action phases.

• The Bleed Out Timer: You enter a critical state lasting a number of combat rounds equal to your Will modifier (minimum 1 round). If you are not stabilized before this timer elapses at the end of your turn, your character suffers permanent death.

• Stabilization Protocol: An adjacent conscious ally within 1 pace can spend their Action Phase to perform a medical stabilization check by rolling 1d20 + Will Modifier vs 14. Upon success, you instantly stabilize and revive at 1 HP.

• Execution Rule: Taking any direct damage while Unconscious at 0 HP bypasses the bleed-out timer and causes immediate permanent death.

6. ARMOR & INVENTORY CAPACITY

Armor absorbs incoming damage by directly subtracting points from flat enemy hits whenever an active Evasion roll fails.

Heavy ArmorDR -2Requires 3 Might. Subtracts 2 points from all incoming physical damage taken.

Inventory Limit: A character can active-equip and carry a maximum number of item slots equal to 4 + Might modifier. Exceeding this carrying capacity causes Encumbrance, inflicting Disadvantage on all physical action rolls and reducing movement paces by half.

THE WARDEN

Narrative Role: The unmovable shield of the party. You secure physical space, hold the line, and endure what others cannot survive.

  • L1 Specialty: Vanguard Guard (Passive): End Move Phase adjacent to an ally to grant them Advantage on Evasion rolls while you remain conscious.
  • L1: Cleave (Heavy Weapon): After a hit, deal 2 flat damage to an adjacent secondary foe within 1 pace.
  • L2: Iron Stance [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP during Action Phase. Reduce all incoming flat damage by 2 points until your next turn (min 1).
  • L3: Brutal Force [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP on hit to add Might modifier to flat damage a second time.
  • L4: Steel Stance [2 RP]: Spend 2 RP during Action Phase. Reduce all incoming flat damage by 4 points until next turn (min 1).
  • L5: Unstoppable Defiance [2 RP]: When dropped to 0 HP, spend 2 RP to remain standing at 1 HP.
  • L6: Hardened Conditioning (Passive): Advantage on Might and Will checks vs environmental weather; rest in heavy armor without penalty.
  • L7: Sunder / Disarm [2 RP]: Spend 2 RP on hit to wrench target's weapon away OR permanently reduce target's Damage Reduction by 2.
  • L8: Brawl Mastery [4 RP]: Spend 4 RP on hit to grapple foe as physical cover (+Advantage on Evasion) OR pin a beast to remove its Action Phase.
Tactical Tip: Your lower Agility modifier makes dodging incoming attacks difficult. Rely heavily on combining Active Guard with your Shield and deploying Iron Stance to soak heavy flat hits, converting yourself into an impassable chokepoint that shields your party.

THE BREAKER

Narrative Role: The aggressive entry specialist. You dictate battlefield positioning and punish enemies through sheer momentum.

  • L1 Specialty: Breacher (Passive): Never suffer movement penalties kicking open doors or smashing barricades.
  • L1: Drive Back [1 RP]: On Heavy Melee hit, shove target 1 pace back. Wall collision deals +2 crush damage.
  • L2: Adrenaline Surge [2 RP]: Double total movement paces for this turn.
  • L3: Concussive Blow [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP on hit. Target passes Might check vs 14 or becomes Dazed.
  • L4: Linebreaker [2 RP]: Move directly through enemy spaces, forcing them 1 pace aside (backed against wall = 2 crush damage).
  • L5: Relentless Assault [3 RP]: Spend 3 RP during Action Phase to make a second attack against a different adjacent target.
  • L6: Pack Mule (Passive): Maximum active inventory slots permanently increased by 2.
  • L7: Heave [2 RP]: Lift and throw heavy object up to 2 paces. Target passes Agility check vs 14 or takes 4 flat damage and falls Prone.
  • L8: Crash the Gates [4 RP]: Smash barrier on Move Phase to take immediate Action Phase with Advantage against unaware targets.
Tactical Tip: Battlefield geometry is your primary weapon. Continually position yourself to use Drive Back and Linebreaker to slam foes directly into walls or physical obstacles, granting free bonus crush damage while displacing enemy formations.

THE PROWLER

Narrative Role: The scalpel. Bypassing security, flanking, and executing devastating close-quarters strikes.

  • L1 Specialty: Twin Fangs (Passive): Dual-wielding Light Melee adds +1 to flat damage output.
  • L1: Backstab [1 RP]: Positioned behind an engaged target, spend 1 RP for Advantage on attack and +2 flat damage.
  • L2: Prowler's Eye [1 RP]: Scan 3-pace area with Advantage vs 14 to spot hidden hazards and mechanisms.
  • L3: Nerve Strike [2 RP]: Spend 2 RP on hit. Target passes Might check vs 14 or becomes Pinned.
  • L4: Shadow Strike [2 RP]: Strike from concealment with Advantage; add Agility mod to flat damage a second time.
  • L5: Absolute Camouflage [3 RP]: In cover, enemies suffer Disadvantage on perception and attack rolls against you.
  • L6: Ghost Step (Passive): Advantage on silent movement; leave no physical footprints on natural terrain.
  • L7: Hamstring [2 RP]: Sever a tendon on hit; target movement permanently reduced to 1 pace until treated.
  • L8: Lethal Strike [4 RP]: Strike unaware non-boss target from behind to bypass armor and instantly drop them to 0 HP.
Tactical Tip: You operate as a lethal glass cannon. Combine Absolute Camouflage with Shadow Strike to consistently roll attacks with Advantage while forcing opponents to suffer Disadvantage when targeting your Evasion.

Guide: "The armored blast door seals shut. Two Elite enforcers take defensive aim from behind a reinforced steel barricade."

Warden: "I'll spend my Action Phase to enter Active Guard with my shield and use my Move Phase to position myself adjacent to the Saboteur."

THE MARKSMAN

Narrative Role: Ranged overwatch. Securing angles, controlling enemy movement, and eliminating targets from afar.

  • L1 Specialty: Aimed Shot (Passive): Forfeit Move Phase to gain Advantage on your Ranged attack roll vs 14.
  • L1: Rig Chokepoint [1 RP]: Set tripwire in adjacent space; first enemy entering has movement reduced to 0 for that turn.
  • L2: Pinning Volley [2 RP]: Hail of fire at chokepoint; first enemy passing through automatically takes flat weapon damage.
  • L3: Crippling Shot [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP before attack. On hit, target's movement is halved on their next turn.
  • L4: Elevated Advantage [1 RP]: Firing from vertical elevation completely ignores target cover bonuses.
  • L5: Piercing Strike [3 RP]: Hit primary target with 2-Handed Ranged weapon and deal equal flat damage to secondary enemy directly behind.
  • L6: Eagle Eye (Passive): Effective range extended by +2 paces across all ranged weapon categories.
  • L7: Funneling Fire [2 RP]: Rapid burst forces target to immediately move 1 pace in a direction of your choice.
  • L8: Perfect Aim [4 RP]: Forfeit Move Phase; roll attack with Advantage, ignore cover, and add Will mod to flat damage.
Tactical Tip: Distance is your primary defense. Prioritize climbing onto elevated perches to activate Elevated Advantage, and deploy Rig Chokepoint to immobilize dangerous Elite targets before they can close into melee range.

THE SABOTEUR

Narrative Role: Environmental manipulator and demolitionist. Disabling gear and exploiting structural weaknesses.

  • L1 Specialty: Saboteur (Passive): Automatically dismantle simple mechanical locks and traps given 1 full minute outside combat.
  • L1: Structural Weakness [1 RP]: Action Phase Will check vs 14; next successful attack against target by any ally ignores target's Damage Reduction.
  • L2: Blinding Debris [1 RP]: Throw ash/debris; Agility check vs 14 grants the target ally Advantage on their next Evasion roll against that enemy.
  • L3: Cripple Target [2 RP]: Action Phase check vs 14 forces target to grant Advantage on all player Evasion rolls against it until it forfeits an Action Phase to recover.
  • L4: Rig Trap [2 RP]: Rig environmental object; Agility check vs 14 drops object for 8 flat damage and knocks Prone.
  • L5: Master of Ruin [3 RP]: Exploit structural flaws in building/vehicle to cause localized physical collapse.
  • L6: Scavenger's Eye (Passive): Identify mechanical components to salvage usable tools from disabled technology.
  • L7: Noxious Irritant [2 RP]: Throw chemical vial at 1-pace spot; enemies starting turn in space grant Advantage on all player attacks and Evasion rolls.
  • L8: Volatile Reaction [4 RP]: Trigger explosive hazard dealing 10 flat damage and Dazed to everything within 2 paces.
Tactical Tip: Your focus is battlefield debuffing. Use Blinding Debris to protect fragile allies, and target high-DR boss enemies with Structural Weakness so your heavy melee allies can strike for full flat damage.

THE SURVIVALIST

Narrative Role: Field medic and tracker. Keeping squad alive through grueling conditions.

  • L1 Specialty: Combat Medic (Passive): Stabilize dying ally within 1 pace using Move Phase (Will check vs 14).
  • L1: Drag to Safety [1 RP]: Grab unconscious or prone ally during Move Phase and drag them full movement paces.
  • L2: Aggressive Rouse [1 RP]: Action Phase instantly removes one physical condition (like Dazed) from an adjacent ally.
  • L3: Pressure Dressing [2 RP]: Bind wound; padding reduces incoming damage of very next attack hitting ally by 3 points.
  • L4: Forage [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP outside combat to automatically locate sustenance to sustain party for one day.
  • L5: Field Surgery [3 RP]: Short Breather procedure restores ally HP equal to twice your Will modifier.
  • L6: Iron Gut (Passive): Advantage vs natural toxins/diseases; require half standard daily rations.
  • L7: Potent Stimulant [2 RP]: Administer brew; ally regains 1 RP and +2 flat damage on next attack (takes 2 crash damage at combat end).
  • L8: Extreme Triage [4 RP]: Save dying ally from lethal hit; instantly wake at 1 HP with permanent maiming.
Tactical Tip: You control the team's combat endurance. Apply Pressure Dressing preemptively onto front-line allies before heavy clashes, and reserve Potent Stimulant to boost allies when bursting down high-HP Boss targets.

THE GUIDE'S MANUAL

1. ADJUDICATING THE THRESHOLD (14)

The Guide never rolls to determine task difficulties or enemy attacks. All player actions, checks, and evasions strictly target the static **Universal Baseline Threshold of 14**.

Difficulty is adjusted dynamically using narrative conditions:

  • Grant Advantage (Roll 2d20, keep high): When players leverage high ground, exploit tactical surprises, double-team isolated foes, or act with ideal tools in favorable contexts.
  • Impose Disadvantage (Roll 2d20, keep low): When players act under severe physical strain, fight on slick ice, suffer from blindness, or target heavily concealed threats.

2. ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARD DAMAGE

Environmental dangers bypass physical armor DR unless explicit hard cover is established. Damage is flat and static based on severity:

  • Minor Hazard (5 Flat Damage): Minor toxins, barbed wire scrapes, sprained ankles, shallow falls, minor electrical sparks.
  • Major Hazard (10 Flat Damage): Deep laceration traps, heavy kinetic deadfalls, acid baths, high-voltage shocks, heavy falling debris.
  • Lethal Hazard (15 Flat Damage): Point-blank fuel explosions, massive cave-ins, magma submersion, planetary atmospheric depressurization.

3. THREAT TIERS & PLAYER-FACING STATS

Combat Traits & Evasion Rules:

  • Tier 1 & 2 (Minions & Standard): Require standard player Evasion rolls vs 14. Minions are designed to be dispatched in a single hit.
  • Tier 3 (Elite): Hard Target: Player attacks against Elites suffer Disadvantage unless the player strikes from ambush or concealment. Tactical: Forces Disadvantage on player Evasion rolls if the target player is Prone or Dazed.
  • Tier 4 (Boss / Leader): Legendary Defense: All player attacks naturally suffer Disadvantage. Frenzy: Bosses possess 2 Actions per turn, forcing target players to roll Evasion twice per round.

LEVEL SCALING BRACKETS: The baseline threat stats above are calibrated for Level 1–2 parties. Scale encounters for higher-level parties using these flat modifiers:

  • Veteran Party (Levels 3–5): All enemies gain +5 HP and +1 Damage. (Bosses gain +15 HP).
  • Apex Party (Levels 6–8): All enemies gain +10 HP, +2 Damage, and +1 DR. (Bosses gain +30 HP).

4. ENEMY MODIFIERS & ENCOUNTER SCALING

Construct balanced combat encounters for a standard 4-player party using these modular templates:

  • Light Skirmish: 4 Minions, 2 Standards
  • Tactical Clash: 3 Minions, 2 Standards, 1 Elite
  • Heavy Assault: 4 Standards, 2 Elites
  • Apex Boss Fight: 5 Minions, 1 Boss

General Enemy Modifiers:

  • Heavy / Armored: Add -1 Damage Reduction to enemy (halve their total movement paces).
  • Agile / Skirmisher: Player rolls Evasion with Disadvantage (+2 Movement paces to enemy, subtract 2 from enemy HP).
  • Lethal / Sniper: Add +2 to incoming Flat Damage (subtract 5 from enemy HP).
  • Morale Test: When an enemy group loses half their total numbers or their squad leader dies, the Guide rolls 1d20. On a roll of 10 or lower, surviving foes rout, retreat, or surrender.
  • Boss Escalation Phase: When a Tier 4 Boss drops below 50% HP, instantly trigger an environmental escalation: spawn 1d4 reinforcing Minions or activate a Major Environmental Hazard.

5. TACTICAL, ENVIRONMENTAL & VISIBILITY RULES

  • Partial Cover: Crouching behind barricades or crates grants Advantage on Evasion Rolls against incoming Ranged attacks.
  • Full Cover: Characters behind complete solid physical barriers cannot be targeted by ranged strikes until enemies maneuver around the barrier.
  • Visibility & Darkness: Dim Light or dense smoke imposes Disadvantage on Perception and Ranged attacks. Pitch Black conditions prevent targeting foes beyond 1 pace and impose Disadvantage on all Evasion rolls.
  • Status Conditions:
    • Prone: Must spend your Move Phase to stand back up.
    • Dazed: Roll your next action with Disadvantage; total movement paces are halved.
    • Pinned: Forfeit your next Action Phase completely.
    • Stunned: Forfeit both your Action Phase and Move Phase on your next turn.
  • Environmental Physics: Falling greater than 2 paces inflicts 2 flat damage per pace fallen. Traversing difficult terrain halves total movement.

6. REST, RECOVERY & REWARDING ROLEPLAY

  • Short Breather (10–15 Minutes): Catch your breath following a fight. Recover HP equal to your Might modifier, and RP equal to your Will modifier (minimum 1 each).
  • Rough Sleep (6–8 Hours): Resting in hostile or dangerous territory. Recover HP and RP equal to your total combined core Attributes.
  • Sanctuary Rest (24 Hours): Resting inside a safe, fortified settlement or secure sanctuary. Completely restores all HP and RP to maximum capacity.
  • Rewarding Resolute Points (RP): The Guide should grant +1 RP to players when they act on their personal Drive to their own clear detriment, execute exceptional tactical teamwork, or drive dramatic narrative roleplay.

THE GUIDE'S GOLDEN RULE: FAILING FORWARD

When a player fails an Out-of-Combat Check vs 14, avoid letting the narrative grind to a complete halt. Rather than stating "nothing happens," introduce an immediate complication or trade-off. The character successfully picks the electronic lock, but trips a silent security alarm. They decipher the ancient ritual rune, but suffer a mental shock. They repair the vehicle drive, but burn out a rare component. Every failed roll must advance the story with fresh tension.

APPENDIX: UNIVERSAL GENRE ADAPTATION

Easy Play D20 functions as a universal narrative chassis. Because all mechanics rely on static flat damage, uniform baseline thresholds, and abstract **Loot Tags** rather than complex currency math, the system adapts instantly to any setting. Below are guidelines for reskinning gear, economy, and magic across various genres.

1. WEALTH, LOOT & NARRATIVE ECONOMY

Wealth is tracked narratively on the Character Sheet using **Loot Tags** rather than counting individual coins or credits. Guide rewards players with setting-appropriate Loot Tags. Players trade these tags in settlements to secure safe Sanctuary Rests, restock gear, purchase bribes, or secure local favors.

2. ADAPTING EQUIPMENT & ARMORY

Armory mechanics remain mathematically identical across all settings. Reskin item names during Character Creation to match your genre:

  • Light Armor (DR -1): Leather jerkin (Fantasy), Kevlar weave (Cyberpunk), tactical vest (Urban Fantasy), scrap vest (Post-Apoc).
  • Heavy Armor (DR -2): Full plate mail (Fantasy), Powered Exosuit (Sci-Fi), Steam-powered rig (Steampunk), reinforced combat shell (Cyberpunk).
  • Active Guard (Shield): Wooden tower shield (Fantasy), hard-light energy barrier (Sci-Fi), steel riot shield (Cyberpunk/Modern).
  • Light Melee (3 Flat Dmg): Shortsword (Fantasy), vibro-blade (Cyberpunk), wrench (Post-Apoc), hunting knife (Space Western).
  • Heavy Melee (5 Flat Dmg): Greataxe (Fantasy), kinetic hammer (Sci-Fi), sledgehammer (Steampunk), power sword (Grimdark).
  • Ranged Weapon (2 Flat Dmg): Longbow (Fantasy), plasma rifle (Sci-Fi), lever-action repeater (Space Western), bolt-action sniper (Modern).

3. CONSUMABLE & UTILITY LOOT MECHANICS

Avoid creating complex custom rules for every item found. Instead, assign setting-flavored consumables to one of three standardized mechanical templates. Using any consumable during combat costs an Action Phase:

  • Restoratives (Potions, Hyper-Med Kits, Healing Tinctures, Stim-Packs): Instantly restores 5 HP, regains 1 RP, OR clears one physical condition (such as Dazed or Pinned).
  • Enhancers (Black Market Ammo, Experimental Serums, Plasma Charges, Weapon Oils): Grants +2 Flat Damage to your next successful attack, OR forces your next attack to completely ignore the target's Armor DR.
  • Utility & Survival (Warding Components, Scavenged Parts, Rations, Lockpicks): Grants Advantage on a specific Out-of-Combat check (e.g., bypassing a security lock, banishing an entity), OR fulfills the food/water requirements needed to safely complete a Rough Sleep or Sanctuary Rest without suffering environmental hazard damage.

4. MAGIC, PSIONICS, CYBERWARE & EXTRAORDINARY ABILITIES

Magic and extraordinary powers are handled through narrative reskinning rather than complex spell slot tracking. A Wizard, Psion, or Cybernetic Mercenary utilizes the exact same class features and weapon math, dressed in setting flavor:

  • Spells as Weapons: A Wizard's "Ranged Weapon" is a Firebolt or Lightning Arc. A Psion's "Heavy Melee" strike is a telekinetic force-crush. A Cyborg's "Ranged/Thrown" weapon is an arm-mounted micro-missile.
  • Abilities as Magic: A Breaker's Concussive Blow can be described as a kinetic shockwave spell, an explosive breaching charge, or a hydraulic cyber-punch. A Saboteur's Structural Weakness can be narrated as a disintegrating magic hex or a targeted armor-corroding acid vial.

5. VEHICLES, MOUNTS & CHASE SEQUENCES

When operating a vehicle or riding a mount (such as a starfighter, a galloping horse, an armored rover, or a hover-skiff), your character's movement capacity equals the vehicle's movement speed in paces. During high-speed chase sequences or hazardous piloting, the driver must pass an Agility Check vs 14 to maintain control; failure results in a crash, inflicting Major Environmental Hazard Damage (10 Flat Damage) to all passengers. Abilities like Master of Ruin or Rig Trap can be used to target enemy vehicle engines directly to force crashes.
