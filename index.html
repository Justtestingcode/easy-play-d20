EASY PLAY D20 — SYSTEM CORE & ARMORY

One player acts as the Guide; everyone else plays an Adventurer. You need a 20-sided die (d20) and these rules.

1. THE ACTION ENGINE (FLAT 14 THRESHOLD)

All risky tasks, skill attempts, attacks, and evasions target a Universal Baseline Threshold of 14.

  • How to Roll: Roll 1d20 + Attribute Modifier. Meet or exceed 14 to succeed.
  • Rule of 20: A Natural 20 in combat is an Overdrive Strike (add Attribute Mod to damage twice). Outside combat, it is an overwhelming success. A Natural 1 is always a critical failure.
THE TWIN DIE ENGINE
Spend 1 Resolute Point (RP) before any roll to buy Advantage (Roll 2d20, keep highest).
Severe obstacles or environmental hazards impose Disadvantage (Roll 2d20, keep lowest).

2. COMBAT & TIME (PLAYER-FACING)

A "Round" represents 6 seconds of simultaneous action. There are no reactions outside your turn. The Guide never rolls to hit.

  • Initiative: Roll 1d20 + Agility Mod vs 14. Highest goes first. Players win ties.
  • 1. Move Phase: Move up to your total Movement paces (1 pace = ~5 feet).
  • 2. Action Phase: Roll 1d20 + Attribute Mod vs 14 to attack. Hit = deal flat damage directly to enemy HP.
  • 3. Evasion Phase (Enemy Turn): When targeted, roll 1d20 + Agility Mod vs 14. Success (14+) = 0 damage. Failure (<14) = take incoming damage minus your Armor DR.
  • Dying (0 HP): Fall unconscious. An ally must pass a Will check vs 14 as an Action within 1 pace to stabilize you, or you die within hours equal to your Will modifier (min 1). Direct damage at 0 HP causes instant death.

THE WARDEN

Narrative Role: The unmovable shield of the party. You secure physical space, hold the line, and endure what others cannot survive.

  • L1 Specialty: Vanguard Guard (Passive): End Move Phase adjacent to an ally to grant them Advantage on Evasion rolls while you remain conscious.
  • L1: Cleave (Heavy Weapon): After a hit, deal 2 flat damage to an adjacent secondary foe within 1 pace.
  • L2: Iron Stance [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP during Action Phase. Reduce all incoming flat damage by 2 points until your next turn (min 1).
  • L3: Brutal Force [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP on hit to add Might modifier to flat damage a second time.
  • L4: Steel Stance [2 RP]: Spend 2 RP during Action Phase. Reduce all incoming flat damage by 4 points until next turn (min 1).
  • L5: Unstoppable Defiance [2 RP]: When dropped to 0 HP, spend 2 RP to remain standing at 1 HP.
  • L6: Hardened Conditioning (Passive): Advantage on Might and Will checks vs environmental weather; rest in heavy armor without penalty.
  • L7: Sunder / Disarm [2 RP]: Spend 2 RP on hit to wrench target's weapon away OR permanently reduce target's Damage Reduction by 2.
  • L8: Brawl Mastery [4 RP]: Spend 4 RP on hit to grapple foe as physical cover (+Advantage on Evasion) OR pin a beast to remove its Action Phase.

THE BREAKER

Narrative Role: The aggressive entry specialist. You dictate battlefield positioning and punish enemies through sheer momentum.

  • L1 Specialty: Breacher (Passive): Never suffer movement penalties kicking open doors or smashing barricades.
  • L1: Drive Back [1 RP]: On Heavy Melee hit, shove target 1 pace back. Wall collision deals +2 crush damage.
  • L2: Adrenaline Surge [2 RP]: Double total movement paces for this turn.
  • L3: Concussive Blow [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP on hit. Target passes Might check vs 14 or becomes Dazed.
  • L4: Linebreaker [2 RP]: Move directly through enemy spaces, forcing them 1 pace aside (backed against wall = 2 crush damage).
  • L5: Relentless Assault [3 RP]: Spend 3 RP during Action Phase to make a second attack against a different adjacent target.
  • L6: Pack Mule (Passive): Maximum active inventory slots permanently increased by 2.
  • L7: Heave [2 RP]: Lift and throw heavy object up to 2 paces. Target passes Agility check vs 14 or takes 4 flat damage and falls Prone.
  • L8: Crash the Gates [4 RP]: Smash barrier on Move Phase to take immediate Action Phase with Advantage against unaware targets.

THE PROWLER

Narrative Role: The scalpel. Bypassing security, flanking, and executing devastating close-quarters strikes.

  • L1 Specialty: Twin Fangs (Passive): Dual-wielding Light Melee adds +1 to flat damage output.
  • L1: Backstab [1 RP]: Positioned behind an engaged target, spend 1 RP for Advantage on attack and +2 flat damage.
  • L2: Prowler's Eye [1 RP]: Scan 3-pace area with Advantage vs 14 to spot hidden hazards and mechanisms.
  • L3: Nerve Strike [2 RP]: Spend 2 RP on hit. Target passes Might check vs 14 or becomes Pinned.
  • L4: Shadow Strike [2 RP]: Strike from concealment with Advantage; add Agility mod to flat damage a second time.
  • L5: Absolute Camouflage [3 RP]: In cover, enemies suffer Disadvantage on perception and attack rolls against you.
  • L6: Ghost Step (Passive): Advantage on silent movement; leave no physical footprints on natural terrain.
  • L7: Hamstring [2 RP]: Sever a tendon on hit; target movement permanently reduced to 1 pace until treated.
  • L8: Lethal Strike [4 RP]: Strike unaware non-boss target from behind to bypass armor and instantly drop them to 0 HP.

THE MARKSMAN

Narrative Role: Ranged overwatch. Securing angles, controlling enemy movement, and eliminating targets from afar.

  • L1 Specialty: Aimed Shot (Passive): Forfeit Move Phase to gain Advantage on your Ranged attack roll vs 14.
  • L1: Rig Chokepoint [1 RP]: Set tripwire in adjacent space; first enemy entering has movement reduced to 0 for that turn.
  • L2: Pinning Volley [2 RP]: Hail of fire at chokepoint; first enemy passing through automatically takes flat weapon damage.
  • L3: Crippling Shot [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP before attack. On hit, target's movement is halved on their next turn.
  • L4: Elevated Advantage [1 RP]: Firing from vertical elevation completely ignores target cover bonuses.
  • L5: Piercing Strike [3 RP]: Hit primary target with 2-Handed Ranged weapon and deal equal flat damage to secondary enemy directly behind.
  • L6: Eagle Eye (Passive): Effective range extended by +2 paces across all ranged weapon categories.
  • L7: Funneling Fire [2 RP]: Rapid burst forces target to immediately move 1 pace in a direction of your choice.
  • L8: Perfect Aim [4 RP]: Forfeit Move Phase; roll attack with Advantage, ignore cover, and add Will mod to flat damage.

THE SABOTEUR

Narrative Role: Environmental manipulator and demolitionist. Disabling gear and exploiting structural weaknesses.

  • L1 Specialty: Saboteur (Passive): Automatically dismantle simple mechanical locks and traps given 1 full minute outside combat.
  • L1: Structural Weakness [1 RP]: Action Phase Will check vs 14; next successful attack against target by any ally ignores target's Damage Reduction.
  • L2: Blinding Debris [1 RP]: Throw ash/debris; Agility check vs 14 grants the target ally Advantage on their next Evasion roll against that enemy.
  • L3: Cripple Target [2 RP]: Action Phase check vs 14 forces target to grant Advantage on all player Evasion rolls against it until it forfeits an Action Phase to recover.
  • L4: Rig Trap [2 RP]: Rig environmental object; Agility check vs 14 drops object for 8 flat damage and knocks Prone.
  • L5: Master of Ruin [3 RP]: Exploit structural flaws in building/vehicle to cause localized physical collapse.
  • L6: Scavenger's Eye (Passive): Identify mechanical components to salvage usable tools from disabled technology.
  • L7: Noxious Irritant [2 RP]: Throw chemical vial at 1-pace spot; enemies starting turn in space grant Advantage on all player attacks and Evasion rolls.
  • L8: Volatile Reaction [4 RP]: Trigger explosive hazard dealing 10 flat damage and Dazed to everything within 2 paces.

THE SURVIVALIST

Narrative Role: Field medic and tracker. Keeping squad alive through grueling conditions.

  • L1 Specialty: Combat Medic (Passive): Stabilize dying ally within 1 pace using Move Phase (Will check vs 14).
  • L1: Drag to Safety [1 RP]: Grab unconscious or prone ally during Move Phase and drag them full movement paces.
  • L2: Aggressive Rouse [1 RP]: Action Phase instantly removes one physical condition (like Dazed) from an adjacent ally.
  • L3: Pressure Dressing [2 RP]: Bind wound; padding reduces incoming damage of very next attack hitting ally by 3 points.
  • L4: Forage [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP outside combat to automatically locate sustenance to sustain party for one day.
  • L5: Field Surgery [3 RP]: Short Breather procedure restores ally HP equal to twice your Will modifier.
  • L6: Iron Gut (Passive): Advantage vs natural toxins/diseases; require half standard daily rations.
  • L7: Potent Stimulant [2 RP]: Administer brew; ally regains 1 RP and +2 flat damage on next attack (takes 2 crash damage at combat end).
  • L8: Extreme Triage [4 RP]: Save dying ally from lethal hit; instantly wake at 1 HP with permanent maiming.

THE GUIDE'S MANUAL

1. ADJUDICATING THE THRESHOLD (14)

The Guide never rolls for difficulty or monster attacks. All player checks target 14. Adjust difficulty using Advantage (favorable/easy context) or Disadvantage (extreme hazards/heavy opposition).

2. ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARD DAMAGE

  • Minor Hazard: 2 Flat Damage (Scrapes, minor toxins, sprained ankles).
  • Major Hazard: 5 Flat Damage (Deep cuts, heavy kinetic traps, electrical shocks).
  • Lethal Hazard: 10 Flat Damage (Point-blank explosions, massive collapses).

4. ENEMY MODIFIERS & ENCOUNTER SCALING

Encounter Scaling Examples (For a standard party of 4):

  • Light Skirmish: 4 Minions, 2 Standards
  • Tactical Clash: 3 Minions, 2 Standards, 1 Elite
  • Heavy Assault: 4 Standards, 2 Elites
  • Apex Boss Fight: 5 Minions, 1 Boss

General Modifiers:

  • Heavy / Armored: Add -1 Damage Reduction to enemy (halve movement).
  • Agile / Skirmisher: Player rolls Evasion with Disadvantage (+2 Movement paces, subtract 2 HP).
  • Lethal / Sniper: Add +2 to incoming Flat Damage (subtract 5 HP).
  • Morale Test: When losing half their numbers or leader, roll 1d20. On 10 or lower, surviving enemies flee or surrender.

5. TACTICAL & ENVIRONMENTAL RULES

  • Partial Cover: Crouching behind objects grants Advantage on Evasion Rolls against Ranged attacks.
  • Full Cover: You cannot be targeted until the enemy physically bypasses the cover.
  • Status Conditions:
    • Prone: Spend Move Phase to stand up.
    • Dazed: Disadvantage on next action roll; movement halved.
    • Pinned: Forfeit next Action Phase.
    • Stunned: Forfeit next Action and Move Phase.
  • Environmental Physics: Falling >2 paces inflicts 2 flat damage per pace fallen. Difficult terrain halves movement.
