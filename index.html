EASY PLAY D20

Welcome to Easy Play D20. In this game, one player acts as the Guide (running the world and enemies), while everyone else plays an Adventurer. You will need a 20-sided die (a d20) and these rules.

1. Heroic Genesis

Before you draw a weapon, you must build your adventurer.

  • Background & Drive: Write down a past career (e.g., Smuggler, Disgraced Noble) and a core motivation (e.g., "I must protect the weak").
  • Class: Choose to be a Warrior, Scout, or Adept.
  • Attributes: You begin with a base score of +1 in Might, Agility, and Will. Allocate 4 additional custom points across these modifiers. Attributes cannot be lower than +1 and are capped at a maximum of +4.
  • Vitals: Compute your HP, Defense, RP, and Movement using the Character Sheet.

2. The Action Engine

When your character attempts something risky, the Guide will ask for an action roll.

  • How Many Dice: Roll 1d20 + matching Attribute Modifier.
  • Why You Roll: To meet or exceed a Target Difficulty set by the Guide (e.g., Easy = 10, Normal = 14, Hard = 18), or an enemy's static Defense Target.
  • How It Resolves: If your total meets or exceeds the target, you succeed. Rule of 20: A Natural 20 is always an automatic HIT, and a Natural 1 is always an automatic MISS.

[!] The Twin Die Engine

Spend 1 Resolute Point (RP) before making any action roll (in or out of combat) to buy Advantage. Roll an explicit second d20 concurrently (2d20) and evaluate using the highest single face value.

If factors impose Disadvantage, you must roll 2d20 and resolve the action using the lowest face value. If you have both, they cancel each other out.

3. Exploration & Social

Attributes govern how you interact with the world outside of combat:

  • Might: Used for physical intimidation, athletic endurance, lifting heavy obstacles, and forcing open doors.
  • Agility: Used for stealth, picking locks, disarming traps, sleight of hand, and dodging hazards or balancing.
  • Will: Used for persuasion, diplomacy, insight (reading intentions), and noticing hidden details.

Background Advantage: If you attempt a task that directly relates to your Background, you automatically gain Advantage on the roll. Your Drive is a pure roleplaying tool to help guide your character's decisions.

4. Tactical Combat Flow

Combat resolves sequentially on your turn.

  • Initiative: Everyone rolls 1d20. The highest roll goes first. On a tie, players act before enemies.
  • 1. Move Phase: Move up to your total Movement paces. (Note: On a tabletop, 1 pace = 1 inch).
  • 2. Action Phase: Roll 1d20 + the matching Attribute Modifier for your weapon against the target's Defense Target. You may only make one attack per turn, regardless of how many weapons you hold.
  • 3. Damage Phase: A successful hit instantly inflicts flat damage based on your weapon.

Hitting 0 HP: If your HP drops to 0, you fall unconscious. You must be stabilized by an ally within a number of hours equal to your Will modifier (minimum 1 hour), or you die. To stabilize you, an ally must use their Action Phase to apply a potion/medkit, or pass a Normal (14) Will check. Once stabilized, you wake up with 1 HP.

5. Rest, Recovery & Downtime

Managing your vitals is crucial, as healing takes time and forces the party to seek safety.

  • Short Breather: A quick pause (10–15 minutes) after a skirmish to bind wounds. Recover HP equal to your Might modifier, and RP equal to your Will modifier (minimum 1 each).
  • Rough Sleep: Resting overnight (6–8 hours) in hostile territory or uncomfortable conditions where you must keep watch. Recover HP and RP equal to the sum of all your Attributes combined (Might + Agility + Will).
  • Sanctuary Rest (Downtime): Spending at least 24 hours in a secure, comfortable location where no one keeps watch. This fully restores all max HP and RP and allows time for roleplay.

6. Leveling Progression

The Guide awards Experience Points (XP) for milestones. You level up cumulatively as you gain XP.

7. The Golden Rule

If a situation arises that is not explicitly covered by this manual, the Guide makes a fair judgment call. Do not let the game stall to argue over physics or logic. Prioritize the story, rely on the core Action Engine, and keep the adventure moving forward.

8. Failing Forward

When an action roll fails, the Guide shouldn't just say "nothing happens." A failure should introduce a new complication, a loss of resources, or a shift in the environment to escalate tension.

ARMORY & EQUIPMENT

Your gear dictates your combat style and tactical flexibility. In Easy Play D20, combat relies on predictable flat damage and strict constraints on what you can hold in your hands. Adapt your arsenal to the threat at hand.

The Hand Limit Rule

Your character only has two hands. How you fill them determines your combat capabilities. Wielding a shield uses 1 hand, completely locking you out from using Heavy Melee Weapons or utilizing the 2-Handed stance for Ranged Weapons.

Armor & Defense

Armor provides a direct, static boost to your Defense Target (DT). You may only wear one type of armor at a time.

Armor Type Bonus to DT Requirements
Light Armor +1 None
Heavy Armor +2 Requires 3 Might
Shield (Passive Wall) +1 Requires 1 Hand

Flat Weapon Damage Engine

To keep combat fast, we do not roll dice to calculate damage. If your attack roll is successful, you deal predictable, flat damage directly to the enemy's Health Points.

Damage Formula

Weapon Base Damage + Attribute Modifier = Total Flat Damage

(Note: Even with negative modifiers or penalties, an attack that successfully hits will always deal a minimum of 1 damage).

Overdrive Strike (Critical Hit)

If your d20 attack roll naturally lands on a 20, you execute an Overdrive Strike. Add your matching Attribute Modifier to the total flat damage a second time.

Wealth & Bartering

Easy Play D20 abstracts exact coin counting into general Wealth, but survival is never free. You must actively scavenge loot, claim bounties, or trade valuables to pay for your basic needs. Booking a safe inn, buying standard consumables, or repairing gear requires spending your hard-earned Wealth. If your pockets are empty, you will be sleeping in the dirt and fighting with whatever you can find.

Other Gear & Inventory

Carrying Capacity & Usage

Your character has a maximum number of Tactical Equipment slots equal to 4 + your Might modifier.

  • Equipped Gear Counts: The armor you wear, the shield on your arm, and the weapons in your hands all take up inventory slots.
  • Consumables: Using an item costs your Action Phase. Note that healing potions and medkits ONLY stabilize dying allies; they do not restore lost HP. True healing requires a Short Breather, a trained doctor, or a medbay.
  • Item Sizes: Meaningful gear takes exactly 1 slot each. Insignificant items (coins, keys) are free to carry.
  • Over-Encumbrance: If you carry more items than your max slots allow, you suffer Disadvantage on all rolls and your Move pace is halved. You may freely drop an item at any time to regain your standard speed and remove the penalty.

UNIFIED PLAYBOOK CODEX

Features execute during your Action Phase unless explicitly marked as a passive structural benefit. Resource expenditures subtract instantly from your current Resolute Points (RP) track.

UNIVERSAL DURATION RULE: Unless an ability explicitly states it affects a single action or attack, any temporary defensive or status effect lasts until the start of your next turn.

The Warrior

Narrative Role: You are the shield of the party. You should be drawing enemy attention, breaking down physical barriers, and leading the charge into the fray.

  • L1 Specialty: Twin Fangs (Passive): When wielding two Light Melee weapons simultaneously, add +1 to your total flat damage output on all successful close-combat strikes. (Requires both hands occupied by weapons).
  • L1: Cleave (Heavy Weapon Only): Immediately following a successful melee strike with a Heavy Weapon, deal your normal flat damage to the primary target, and inflict exactly 2 flat damage to one adjacent secondary foe within 1 pace (or 1 inch). (Requires 2 free hands).
  • L2: Iron Hide [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP during the Damage Phase when you are hit by an attack. You instantly blunt the blow, reducing the damage of that specific attack by 2 points (minimum 1 damage taken).
  • L3: Brutal Force [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP immediately after confirming a successful melee hit. You completely double your Might modifier's contribution to that attack's flat damage calculation.
  • L4: Steel Hide [2 RP]: Spend 2 RP during the Damage Phase when you are hit by an attack. You instantly harden your stance, reducing all incoming damage taken by 4 points (minimum 1 damage taken per hit) until the start of your next turn.
  • L5: Unstoppable Defiance [2 RP]: Spend 2 RP when an attack would reduce your HP to 0; you completely negate the knockout blow, remaining active and standing at exactly 1 HP.

The Scout

Narrative Role: You are the eyes and ears. You should be scouting ahead, disarming traps, finding safe paths, and eliminating priority targets from the shadows.

  • L1: Fade Step (Passive): During your Move Phase, you may freely step directly behind an engaged enemy without penalty or complication.
  • L1: Climber (Passive): Climbing costs standard movement paces instead of double.
  • L1: Backstab [1 RP]: When you are positioned directly behind a target engaged by an active ally from the front, spend 1 RP to gain Advantage (roll 2d20, keep highest) on your attack roll and add +2 to your flat damage. (Requires a Light Melee weapon).
  • L2: Infiltrator's Eye [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP to automatically succeed without needing a Will check, instantly revealing all hidden traps, secret pathways, footprints, or ambushes in your immediate zone.
  • L3 Specialty: Poison Pulse [2 RP]: Spend 2 RP upon landing a successful weapon attack to Stun the target. The Stun status completely skips the target's next action phase and lasts until the start of that target's subsequent turn.
  • L4: Shadow Strike [2 RP]: Spend 2 RP during your Action Phase to strike from absolute concealment. The attack automatically hits without requiring a d20 roll, dealing your flat weapon base damage + your Agility modifier.
  • L5: Vanish [3 RP]: Spend 3 RP when targeted by an enemy attack. You instantly dissolve into environmental cover, breaking line of sight and causing the incoming attack to automatically fail.

The Adept

Narrative Role: You are the wildcard. You should be manipulating the battlefield, discovering hidden secrets, and providing critical utility to your team.

  • L1: Focused Grasp (Passive): Your hands crackle with passive energy. You can make a free close-combat attack at a 1-pace range (or 1 inch) without spending RP. On a successful Will check, you deal flat damage equal to 1 + your Will modifier.
  • L1: Trickery (Passive): You may produce minor sensory effects—such as a brief spark, a sudden shadow, a faint whisper, or a slight draft—freely and automatically without spending RP.
  • L2: Pyrokinesis [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP to instantly channel molecular friction, automatically igniting candles, campfires, torches, or any preprepared highly combustible environmental item within 10 paces (or 10 inches).
  • L3 Specialty: Aegis Shield [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP when an ally in your line of sight is targeted by an attack. You instantly grant them a +2 bonus to their Defense Target against that specific incoming attack.
  • L4: Shatter-Blast [1 RP]: Spend 1 RP to direct a kinetic shockwave at a visible target. On a successful Will check against their Defense Target, you inflict flat damage equal to your Will modifier + 6.
  • L5: Kinetic Storm [3 RP]: Spend 3 RP to release a surge of raw kinetic energy, constructing a protective field extending 2 paces (or 2 inches) outward in all directions from your character. This field automatically blocks all incoming standard projectile attacks until the start of your next turn.
