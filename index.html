QUICK-REF SYSTEM CHECKS (UNIVERSAL THRESHOLD = 14)
• The 14+ Rule: Any check, attack, or defense totaling 14+ after modifiers is a complete success. Anything under 14 is a failure. Always round fractions down (Min 1).
• The Twin Die Engine (Advantage): Spend 1 Force (Overpower) for Advantage on Might checks/physical contests. Spend 1 Reflex (Dodge) for Advantage on Evasion or Agility checks. Spend 1 RP (Focus) for Advantage on Attack rolls or Will checks.
• Universal Reflex Actions: Spend 1 Reflex Point for: Burst Step (+1 Pace) or Quick Stand (Stand from Prone free).
• Action Phase (Attacking): Roll 1d20 + Weapon Attr vs 14 within range (Hard Max 8 Paces for Ranged Weapons). Success deals Flat Damage directly to HP.
• Dying & Bleed Out (0 HP): Counter starts at Will Mod + 1 (Min 1 turn). Decrement by 1 at start of turn. Reaching 0 = permanent death. Ally spending Action Phase passes Will vs 14 to revive at 1 HP.
ACTION PHASE MANEUVERS (NON-ATTACK)
• Active Guard: Raise Shield (forfeits Action AND Move Phase) to gain Advantage on all Evasion rolls until next turn.
• Dash: Forfeit attack to move total Movement paces a second time.
• Assist Ally: Grant an adjacent ally Advantage on their very next check or attack roll.
• Hide / Sneak: Roll Agility vs 14 to gain physical Concealment (Enemies cannot target you with ranged attacks; attacking from Concealment grants Advantage and ends Concealment).
• Interact / Weapon Swap: Pick lock, pull lever, or hand item. Drawing or swapping weapons is a Free Action once per turn during Move Phase.

EASY PLAY D20 - SYSTEM CORE and ARMORY

One participant acts as the Guide; all others play Adventurers. Requires a single 20-sided die (d20) per player and these core rules.

1. HEROIC GENESIS (CHARACTER CREATION)

Building an Adventurer requires four foundational steps:

  • Background and Drive: Record a former trade and a core motivation.
  • Class Selection: Choose 1 Combat Archetype from the Playbook Codex. Your class dictates your battlefield role and determines which unique abilities you unlock as you level up.
  • Attributes: Base score of +1 in Might, Agility, and Will. Allocate +2 custom points across these (Absolute Max +4 EVER per attribute).
  • Derived Vitals:
    • HP: 20 + Might Modifier (Pure base math; gear never touches HP)
    • Force Pool: (Might x 2) + Leveling Bonuses
    • Reflex Pool: (Agility x 2) + Leveling Bonuses
    • Resolute RP: (Will x 2) + Leveling Bonuses
    • Movement: Base 3 Paces + Armor/Shield Speed Mod (Minimum Movement Floor = 1 Pace)

2. THE ACTION ENGINE (FLAT 14)

All risky tasks, skill checks, attack rolls, and evasions target a static Universal Baseline Threshold of 14.

THE 14+ RULE & ROUNDING: Any check, attack, or defense that totals 14 or higher after modifiers is a complete success. Anything under 14 is a failure. Always round fractions down to the nearest whole number (minimum 1 Pace/HP).
  • Resolution: Roll 1d20 + Attribute Modifier. Sum of 14+ succeeds.
  • Critical Hit (Nat 20): Overdrive Strike—add Attribute Mod to flat damage a second time. Ability Stacking: If an ability also adds Attribute Mod a second time, a Nat 20 stacks them for a third addition (Base + Attr Mod x 3).
  • Critical Failure (Nat 1): Automatic miss; you suffer Disadvantage on your very next Evasion roll.
THE TWIN DIE ENGINE & 3-RESOURCE MATRIX
• Overpower (1 Force): Spend 1 Force before rolling to buy Advantage (2d20, keep high) on any Might check or physical contest.
• Dodge (1 Reflex): Spend 1 Reflex before rolling to buy Advantage on any Evasion roll or Agility check.
• Focus (1 RP): Spend 1 RP before rolling to buy Advantage on any Attack roll or Will check.
(Advantage + Disadvantage cancel out).

UNIVERSAL REFLEX ACTIONS: Spend 1 Reflex Point on your turn for Burst Step (+1 Pace) or Quick Stand (Prone recovery without halving movement).

3. COMBAT STRUCTURE and TURN FLOW

Rounds represent 6 seconds of simultaneous action. System is 100% Player-Facing: no out-of-turn reactions.

  • Dynamic Guide Initiative: The Guide rolls 1d20 (+ Tier Mod) to set the encounter's Initiative Target. Players roll 1d20 + Agility vs this target. Meet/beat = Fast Phase (before enemies); lower = Slow Phase (after enemies). Players win ties. Player Turn Order: Players in the same phase (Fast or Slow) may act in any order they choose.
  • 1. Move Phase: Travel up to Movement speed in paces (1 pace = 5 ft / 1 grid square; Min Floor = 1 Pace). Halved movement is rounded down (Min 1 Pace). Drawing or swapping weapons is a Free Action once per turn.
  • 2. Action Phase: Attack, use ability, drink potion, or execute a maneuver (1d20 + Attr vs 14).
  • 3. Evasion Phase: When targeted on enemy turn, roll 1d20 + Armor Evasion Mod vs 14. 14+ = 0 damage. Fail = take enemy flat damage directly.
DEATH and STABILIZATION (0 HP)
At 0 HP you collapse Prone and become Unconscious.
• Bleed Out Timer: Starts at Will Modifier + 1 (Min 1 turn). At the start of your turn while at 0 HP, decrement timer by 1. Reaching 0 causes permanent death.
• Stabilization: Adjacent ally spends Action Phase for Will check vs 14 to revive you at 1 HP.
• Execution: Direct damage taken while at 0 HP causes instant permanent death.

THE WARDEN (3 Force / 2 Reflex / 2 RP)

Narrative Role: The unmovable shield of the party. You secure physical space, hold the line, and endure what others cannot survive.

Recommended Loadout: Heavy Armor (+3 Evade, -2 Paces), Shield (-1 Pace), Light Melee (3 Dmg, 1 Pace Range).

  • L1 Specialty: Vanguard Guard (Passive): If you end your turn adjacent to an ally, grant them Advantage on Evasion rolls until your next turn.
  • L1: Cleave [1 Force]: After a Heavy Weapon hit, spend 1 Force to deal 2 flat damage to an adjacent secondary foe within 1 pace.
  • L2: Iron Stance [1 Reflex]: Spend 1 Reflex during Action Phase. Auto-succeed on your next Evasion roll before your next turn.
  • L3: Brutal Force [1 Force]: Spend 1 Force on hit to add Might modifier to flat damage a second time.
  • L4: Steel Stance [2 RP]: Spend 2 RP during Action Phase. Cap maximum incoming flat damage from the very next successful hit against you at 2 points.
  • L5: Unstoppable Defiance [2 RP]: When dropped to 0 HP, spend 2 RP to remain standing at 1 HP.
  • L6: Hardened Conditioning (Passive): Advantage on Might and Will checks vs environmental weather; rest in heavy armor without penalty.
  • L7: Sunder / Disarm [2 Reflex]: Spend 2 Reflex on hit to wrench target's weapon away OR deal +3 bonus flat damage.
  • L8: Brawl Mastery [4 Force]: Spend 4 Force on hit to grapple foe within 1 pace as physical cover (+Advantage on Evasion) OR pin a beast to remove its Action Phase.
Rule Clarification: Active Guard with a Shield uses both your Action and Move Phases. Vanguard Guard triggers at the end of your turn as long as you are standing next to an ally. Minimum movement speed is 1 Pace.

THE BREAKER (3 Force / 2 Reflex / 2 RP)

Narrative Role: The aggressive entry specialist. You dictate battlefield positioning and punish enemies through sheer momentum.

Recommended Loadout: Heavy Melee (4 Dmg, 1 Pace Range), Medium Armor (+2 Evade, -1 Pace), Improvised Tools.

  • L1 Specialty: Breacher (Passive): Never suffer movement penalties kicking open doors or smashing barricades.
  • L1: Drive Back [1 Force]: On Heavy Melee hit, spend 1 Force to shove target 1 pace back. Wall collision deals +2 crush damage.
  • L2: Adrenaline Surge [2 Reflex]: Double total movement paces for this turn.
  • L3: Concussive Blow [1 Force]: Spend 1 Force on hit. Target passes Might check vs 14 or becomes Dazed.
  • L4: Linebreaker [2 Reflex]: Move directly through enemy spaces, forcing them 1 pace aside (backed against wall = 2 crush damage).
  • L5: Relentless Assault [3 RP]: Spend 3 RP during Action Phase to make a second attack against a different adjacent target within 1 pace.
  • L6: Pack Mule (Passive): Maximum active inventory slots permanently increased by 2.
  • L7: Heave [2 Force]: Lift and throw heavy object up to 2 paces. Target passes Agility check vs 14 or takes 4 flat damage and falls Prone.
  • L8: Crash the Gates [4 RP]: Smash barrier on Move Phase to take immediate Action Phase with Advantage against unaware targets.
Rule Clarification: Crush Damage requires the enemy to be physically slammed into a heavy, unmoving barrier, such as a stone wall, a large vehicle, or a massive tree trunk.

THE PROWLER (2 Force / 3 Reflex / 2 RP)

Narrative Role: The scalpel. Bypassing security, flanking, and executing devastating close-quarters strikes.

Recommended Loadout: Twin Light Melee (3 Dmg +1 from Twin Fangs, 1 Pace Range), Unarmored (+1 Pace) or Light Armor (+1 Evade), Ranged/Thrown Daggers (3 Paces Range).

  • L1 Specialty: Twin Fangs (Passive): Dual-wielding Light Melee adds +1 to flat damage output.
  • L1: Backstab [1 Reflex]: Positioned behind an engaged target within 1 pace, spend 1 Reflex for Advantage on attack and +2 flat damage.
  • L2: Prowler's Eye [1 RP]: Scan 3-pace area with Advantage vs 14 to spot hidden hazards and mechanisms.
  • L3: Nerve Strike [2 Reflex]: Spend 2 Reflex on hit. Target passes Might check vs 14 or becomes Grappled.
  • L4: Shadow Strike [2 Reflex]: Strike from concealment with Advantage; add Agility mod to flat damage a second time.
  • L5: Absolute Camouflage [3 RP]: In cover, enemies suffer Disadvantage on perception and attack rolls against you.
  • L6: Ghost Step (Passive): Advantage on silent movement; leave no physical footprints on natural terrain.
  • L7: Hamstring [2 Force]: Sever a tendon on hit; spend 2 Force to permanently reduce target movement to 1 pace until treated.
  • L8: Lethal Strike [4 Force]: Strike unaware non-boss target from behind within 1 pace; spend 4 Force to instantly drop them to 0 HP.
Rule Clarification: To attack from Concealment for Shadow Strike, you must be in dim light, behind full cover, or have successfully used your Action Phase to roll the Hide maneuver on a previous turn. Spending RP on Advantage abilities cancels Boss Legendary Defense down to a standard 1d20 roll and satisfies ability prerequisites.

THE MARKSMAN (2 Force / 2 Reflex / 3 RP)

Narrative Role: Ranged overwatch. Securing angles, controlling enemy movement, and eliminating targets from afar.

Recommended Loadout: Ranged Weapon (2 Dmg, 8 Paces Range Max), Unarmored (+1 Pace) or Light Armor (+1 Evade), Light Melee backup weapon (1 Pace Range).

  • L1 Specialty: Aimed Shot (Passive): Forfeit Move Phase to gain Advantage on your Ranged attack roll vs 14.
  • L1: Rig Chokepoint [1 Reflex]: Set tripwire in adjacent space; first enemy entering has movement reduced to 0 for that turn.
  • L2: Pinning Volley [2 RP]: Hail of fire covering a 2-pace chokepoint up to 8 paces away; first enemy passing through is Pinned Down.
  • L3: Crippling Shot [1 Force]: Spend 1 Force before attack. On hit within range, target's movement is halved on their next turn.
  • L4: Elevated Advantage [1 Reflex]: Scale vertical elevation quickly to fire from high ground, completely ignoring target cover bonuses.
  • L5: Piercing Strike [3 RP]: Hit primary target with 2-Handed Ranged weapon up to 8 paces away and deal equal flat damage to secondary enemy directly behind them.
  • L6: Eagle Eye (Passive): Effective range extended by +2 paces across all ranged weapon categories (Ranged/Thrown = 5 Paces, Ranged Weapon = 10 Paces Max).
  • L7: Funneling Fire [2 Force]: Rapid burst forces target within range to immediately move 1 pace in a direction of your choice.
  • L8: Perfect Aim [4 RP]: Forfeit Move Phase; roll attack up to 8 paces with Advantage, ignore cover, and add Will mod to flat damage.
Rule Clarification: To trigger Elevated Advantage, you must physically be positioned at least 1 full pace (5 feet) higher than your target, such as firing from a rooftop, a staircase, or a hovering vehicle. Hard max range for standard Ranged Weapons is 8 Paces.

THE SABOTEUR (2 Force / 2 Reflex / 3 RP)

Narrative Role: Environmental manipulator and demolitionist. Disabling gear and exploiting structural weaknesses.

Recommended Loadout: Light Melee (3 Dmg, 1 Pace Range), Light Armor (+1 Evade), Ranged/Thrown explosives (3 Paces Range).

  • L1 Specialty: Saboteur (Passive): Automatically dismantle simple mechanical locks and traps given 1 full minute outside combat.
  • L1: Structural Weakness [1 RP]: Action Phase Will check vs 14 against target within 3 paces; next successful attack against target by any ally deals +2 Flat Damage.
  • L2: Blinding Debris [1 Reflex]: Throw ash/debris up to 3 paces; Agility check vs 14 grants target ally Advantage on their next Evasion roll against that enemy.
  • L3: Cripple Target [2 Force]: Wrench armor plates; spend 2 Force to force target within 3 paces to grant Advantage on all player Evasion rolls against it until it forfeits an Action Phase.
  • L4: Rig Trap [2 Reflex]: Rig environmental object; Agility check vs 14 drops object for 8 flat damage and knocks Prone.
  • L5: Master of Ruin [3 RP]: Exploit structural flaws in building/vehicle to cause localized physical collapse.
  • L6: Scavenger's Eye (Passive): Identify mechanical components to salvage usable tools from disabled technology.
  • L7: Noxious Irritant [2 Force]: Throw chemical vial up to 3 paces to coat a 1-pace spot; enemies starting turn in space grant Advantage on all player attacks and Evasion rolls.
  • L8: Volatile Reaction [4 RP]: Trigger explosive hazard dealing 10 flat damage and Dazed to everything within 2 paces.
Rule Clarification: Valid environmental objects for Rig Trap include heavy chandeliers, stacked cargo crates, loose rock formations, or unstable scaffolding directly above the target.

THE SURVIVALIST (2 Force / 2 Reflex / 3 RP)

Narrative Role: Field medic and tracker. Keeping squad alive through grueling conditions.

Recommended Loadout: Ranged/Thrown weapons (1 Dmg, 3 Paces Range), Light Armor (+1 Evade), survival and medical gear.

  • L1 Specialty: Combat Medic (Passive): Stabilize dying ally within 1 pace using Move Phase (Will check vs 14).
  • L1: Drag to Safety [1 Reflex]: Grab unconscious or prone ally during Move Phase and drag them full movement paces.
  • L2: Aggressive Rouse [1 RP]: Action Phase instantly removes one physical condition (like Dazed or Grappled) from an adjacent ally within 1 pace.
  • L3: Pressure Dressing [2 Reflex]: Fast-hand combat bandage on self or adjacent ally restores 3 HP immediately (up to Max HP) OR grants Advantage on their next Evasion roll.
  • L4: Forage [1 Force]: Spend 1 Force outside combat to automatically locate sustenance to sustain party for one day.
  • L5: Field Surgery [3 RP]: Short Breather procedure restores ally HP equal to twice your Will modifier.
  • L6: Iron Gut (Passive): Advantage vs natural toxins/diseases; require half standard daily rations.
  • L7: Potent Stimulant [2 Force]: Administer brew to self or adjacent ally; spend 2 Force so target regains 1 RP and +2 flat damage on next attack (takes 2 crash damage at combat end).
  • L8: Extreme Triage [4 RP]: Save dying ally within 1 pace from lethal hit; instantly wake at 1 HP with permanent maiming.

THE GUIDE'S MANUAL

1. ADJUDICATING THRESHOLDS & SOCIAL CHECKS

All player actions target the static Universal Baseline Threshold of 14.

  • Grant Advantage: High ground, tactical surprise, or ideal tools.
  • Impose Disadvantage: Heavy strain, slick ice, or full cover.
  • Checks: Will vs 14 (Social), Agility vs 14 (Stealth), Might vs 14 (Force).
WHEN DOES THE GUIDE ROLL A D20?
1. Initiative Target: Roll 1d20 (+ Tier Mod) at combat start.
2. Morale Checks: Roll 1d20 when enemy squad loses 50% numbers/leader.
3. Boss Escalation: Roll 1d20 at 50% Boss HP (1-10: 2 Minions, 11-20: Hazard).
4. Failing Forward: Roll 1d20 for narrative complication severity.

5. TACTICAL CONDITIONS & REST

Status Conditions (Players & Enemies):

  • Prone: Halves movement speed (round down, min 1 Pace). All physical attacks and maneuver rolls suffer Disadvantage.
  • Dazed: Suffer Disadvantage on all rolls (attacks, checks, evasions) until end of next turn; movement speed is halved (min 1 Pace).
  • Grappled (Physical): Completely immobilized (Movement = 0). Must spend Action Phase and pass Might check vs 14 to break free.
  • Pinned Down (Suppressive): Trapped behind cover. Moving outside cover triggers automatic enemy flat damage. Spend Move Phase OR pass Agility/Will check vs 14 to clear.
  • Stunned: Completely incapacitated. Forfeits all Move and Action Phases on next turn (can still roll Evasion when attacked).

Rest & Recovery Mechanics:

  • Short Breather (10 min): Regain HP equal to (Might Mod x 2), Force equal to Might Mod, Reflex equal to Agility Mod, and RP equal to Will Mod.
  • Rough Sleep (Overnight in Wilds): Regain Half Max HP (round down); completely refuels Force, Reflex, and RP pools to max.
  • Sanctuary Rest (Town/Base): Full 100% restore of HP, Force, Reflex, and RP + clears all conditions. Cost: 1 Minor Tag per player.
THE GUIDE'S GOLDEN RULE: FAILING FORWARD
On failed checks vs 14, introduce narrative complications (noise, tool break, 5 Flat Dmg). Roll 1d20 for severity (1-10 Minor / 11-20 Major).

APPENDIX: UNIVERSAL GENRE ADAPTATION

Easy Play D20 functions as a universal narrative chassis. Because all mechanics rely on static flat damage, uniform baseline thresholds, and abstract Loot Tags rather than complex currency math, the system adapts instantly to any setting.

1. WEALTH, LOOT and NARRATIVE ECONOMY

Wealth is tracked on the Character Sheet using Loot Tags rather than individual coins or credits. The Guide awards setting-appropriate Loot Tags after combat or exploration. Players trade these tags in settlements for Sanctuary Rests (1 Minor Tag per player), gear, bribes, or local favors.

2. ADAPTING THE UNIVERSAL THRESHOLD

The baseline Threshold of 14 is mathematically tuned to provide a gritty, highly tactical combat experience where characters will miss attacks and fail dodges roughly half the time without spending RP. However, the Guide can universally adjust this number to fit the desired tone of the campaign:

  • Super-Heroic Action (Target 12): Lowers the barrier to entry, ensuring players succeed more often without spending RP. Perfect for fast-paced, pulp-action settings.
  • Survival Horror (Target 16): Drastically increases the failure rate, forcing players to burn RP constantly just to survive standard encounters. Best for grimdark or horror campaigns.

3. ADAPTING EQUIPMENT and ARMORY

Armory mechanics remain mathematically identical across all settings. Reskin item names during Character Creation to fit the narrative:

  • Unarmored (+1 Pace): Civilian clothes (Fantasy/Modern), street clothes (Cyberpunk), flight suit (Sci-Fi).
  • Light Armor (+1 Evade): Leather jerkin (Fantasy), Kevlar weave (Cyberpunk), tactical vest (Urban Fantasy), scrap vest (Post-Apoc).
  • Medium Armor (+2 Evade): Chainmail / Reinforced coat (Fantasy), combat weave (Cyberpunk), armored duster (Space Western).
  • Heavy Armor (+3 Evade): Full plate mail (Fantasy), Powered Exosuit (Sci-Fi), Steam-powered rig (Steampunk), combat shell (Cyberpunk).
  • Active Guard (Shield): Wooden tower shield (Fantasy), hard-light barrier (Sci-Fi), steel riot shield (Cyberpunk/Modern).
  • Light Melee (3 Flat Dmg, 1 Pace): Shortsword (Fantasy), vibro-blade (Cyberpunk), wrench (Post-Apoc), hunting knife (Space Western).
  • Heavy Melee (4 Flat Dmg, 1 Pace): Greataxe (Fantasy), kinetic hammer (Sci-Fi), sledgehammer (Steampunk), power sword (Grimdark).
  • Ranged Weapon (2 Flat Dmg, 8 Paces Max): Longbow (Fantasy), plasma rifle (Sci-Fi), lever-action repeater (Space Western), sniper (Modern).

4. CONSUMABLE and UTILITY LOOT MECHANICS

Assign setting-flavored consumables to one of three standardized mechanical templates. Using any consumable during combat costs an Action Phase:

  • Restoratives (Potions, Hyper-Med Kits, Stim-Packs): Restores 5 HP, regains 1 RP/Reflex/Force, OR clears one physical condition (such as Dazed or Grappled).
  • Enhancers (Black Market Ammo, Experimental Serums, Plasma Charges): Grants +2 Flat Damage to your next hit, OR forces your next attack to ignore cover.
  • Utility and Survival (Warding Components, Scavenged Parts, Rations): Grants Advantage on a specific Out-of-Combat check, OR fulfills food/water requirements needed to safely complete a Rough Sleep or Sanctuary Rest without taking hazard damage.

5. MAGIC, PSIONICS and CYBERWARE

Magic and extraordinary powers are handled through narrative reskinning. A Wizard, Psion, or Cyborg utilizes the standard class features and weapon math dressed in setting flavor:

  • Spells as Weapons: A Wizard's "Ranged Weapon" (8 Paces Max) is a Firebolt. A Psion's "Heavy Melee" (1 Pace) is a telekinetic force-crush. A Cyborg's "Ranged/Thrown" weapon (3 Paces) is a micro-missile.
  • Abilities as Magic: A Breaker's Concussive Blow can be narrated as a kinetic shockwave spell or hydraulic cyber-punch. A Saboteur's Structural Weakness can be a disintegrating magic hex or acid vial.

6. VEHICLES, MOUNTS and CHASES

When operating a vehicle or riding a mount (starfighter, horse, rover, hover-skiff), your character's movement capacity equals the vehicle's movement speed in paces. Vehicles grant an extra +2 Evasion modifier while occupied. When a vehicle reaches 0 HP, it is destroyed, forcing occupants to suffer a Lethal Hazard (15 Flat Damage). During chase sequences or hazardous piloting, the driver must pass an Agility Check vs 14 to maintain control; failure results in a crash, inflicting Major Environmental Hazard Damage (10 Flat Damage) to all passengers.
