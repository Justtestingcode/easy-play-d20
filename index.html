EASY PLAY D20 — SYSTEM CORE & ARMORY

One player acts as the Guide; everyone else plays an Adventurer. You need a 20-sided die (d20) and these rules.

1. THE ACTION ENGINE (FLAT 14 THRESHOLD)

All risky tasks, skill attempts, attacks, and evasions target a Universal Baseline Threshold of 14.

  • How to Roll: Roll 1d20 + Attribute Modifier. Meet or exceed 14 to succeed.
  • Rule of 20: A Natural 20 in combat is an Overdrive Strike (add Attribute Mod to damage twice). Outside combat, it is an overwhelming success. A Natural 1 is always a critical failure.
THE TWIN DIE ENGINE
Spend 1 Resolute Point (RP) before any roll to buy Advantage (Roll 2d20, keep highest).
Severe obstacles or environmental hazards impose Disadvantage (Roll 2d20, keep lowest).

2. COMBAT & TIME (PLAYER-FACING)

A "Round" represents 6 seconds of simultaneous action. The Guide never rolls to hit.

  • Initiative: Roll 1d20 vs 14. Highest goes first. Players win ties.
  • 1. Move Phase: Move up to your total Movement paces (1 pace = ~5 feet).
  • 2. Action Phase: Roll 1d20 + Attribute Mod vs 14 to attack. Deal flat weapon damage on hit.
  • 3. Evasion Phase (Enemy Turn): When targeted, roll 1d20 + Agility Mod vs 14. Success (14+) = 0 damage. Failure (<14) = take incoming damage minus your Armor DR.
  • Dying (0 HP): Fall unconscious. An ally must pass a Will check vs 14 within 1 pace to stabilize you, or you die within hours equal to your Will modifier. Direct damage at 0 HP causes instant death.

UNIFIED PLAYBOOK CODEX

THE WARDEN

The unmovable shield of the party.

  • L1 Vanguard Guard: End Move Phase adjacent to ally to grant them Advantage on Evasion.
  • L1 Cleave: Heavy Melee hit deals +2 flat damage to adjacent secondary foe.
  • L2 Iron Stance [1 RP]: Reduce incoming damage by 2 until next turn.
  • L3 Brutal Force [1 RP]: Add Might modifier to flat damage a second time.
  • L4 Steel Stance [2 RP]: Reduce incoming damage by 4 until next turn.
  • L5 Unstoppable Defiance [2 RP]: When dropped to 0 HP, stand at 1 HP.
  • L8 Brawl Mastery [4 RP]: Grapple foe as cover (+Adv on Evasion) or pin beast.

THE BREAKER

The aggressive entry specialist.

  • L1 Breacher: Never suffer penalties breaking doors/barricades.
  • L1 Drive Back [1 RP]: Heavy Melee hit shoves target 1 pace back (+2 wall crush damage).
  • L2 Adrenaline Surge [2 RP]: Double movement paces for turn.
  • L3 Concussive Blow [1 RP]: Target passes Might check vs 14 or becomes Dazed.
  • L5 Relentless Assault [3 RP]: Make a second attack against adjacent target.
  • L8 Crash the Gates [4 RP]: Smash barrier to take Action Phase with Advantage against unaware foes.

THE PROWLER

The lethal stealth scalpel.

  • L1 Twin Fangs: Dual-wielding Light Melee adds +1 flat damage.
  • L1 Backstab [1 RP]: Attack from behind for Advantage and +2 damage.
  • L3 Nerve Strike [2 RP]: Target passes Might check vs 14 or becomes Pinned.
  • L4 Shadow Strike [2 RP]: Strike from concealment with Advantage; add Agility mod twice.
  • L8 Lethal Strike [4 RP]: Strike unaware target from behind to bypass armor and instantly drop to 0 HP.

THE MARKSMAN

The ranged overwatch specialist.

  • L1 Aimed Shot: Forfeit Move Phase to gain +2 on Ranged attack vs 14.
  • L2 Pinning Volley [2 RP]: First enemy entering chokepoint automatically takes flat weapon damage.
  • L4 Elevated Advantage [1 RP]: Firing from vertical elevation ignores target cover.
  • L5 Piercing Strike [3 RP]: Hit primary target and deal equal damage to foe directly behind.
  • L8 Perfect Aim [4 RP]: Forfeit Move; roll with Advantage, ignore cover, add Will mod to damage.

THE SABOTEUR

Environmental manipulator and demolitionist.

  • L1 Structural Weakness [1 RP]: Will check vs 14; next hit against target ignores Armor DR.
  • L2 Blinding Debris [1 RP]: Throw ash/debris; Agility check vs 14 inflicts -2 on target's next attack.
  • L4 Rig Trap [2 RP]: Rig hazard; Agility check vs 14 drops object for 8 flat damage and Prone.
  • L8 Volatile Reaction [4 RP]: Trigger explosion for 10 flat damage and Dazed within 2 paces.

THE SURVIVALIST

Field medic and tracker.

  • L1 Combat Medic: Stabilize dying ally within 1 pace using Move Phase (Will check vs 14).
  • L1 Drag to Safety [1 RP]: Grab unconscious ally and drag them full movement paces.
  • L3 Pressure Dressing [2 RP]: Reduce incoming damage of next hit against ally by 3 points.
  • L8 Extreme Triage [4 RP]: Save dying ally from lethal hit; wake at 1 HP permanently maimed.

THE GUIDE'S MANUAL

1. ADJUDICATING THE THRESHOLD (14)

The Guide never rolls for difficulty or monster attacks. All player checks meet or exceed 14. Apply Advantage for favorable circumstances or Disadvantage for severe hazards.

2. HAZARD FLAT DAMAGE

  • Minor Hazard: 2 Flat Damage (Scrapes, minor toxins, sprained ankles).
  • Major Hazard: 5 Flat Damage (Deep cuts, heavy kinetic traps, electrical shocks).
  • Lethal Hazard: 10 Flat Damage (Point-blank explosions, massive collapses).

4. ENEMY MODIFIERS & MORALE

  • Heavy / Armored: Add +1 Damage Reduction to enemy (halve movement).
  • Agile / Skirmisher: Player rolls Evasion with Disadvantage (+2 Movement paces, subtract 2 HP).
  • Lethal / Sniper: Add +2 to incoming Flat Damage (subtract 5 HP).
  • Morale Test: When losing half their numbers or leader, roll 1d20. On 10 or lower, surviving enemies flee or surrender.
