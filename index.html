THE GUIDE'S MANUAL

1. ADJUDICATING THRESHOLDS & SOCIAL CHECKS

All player actions strictly target the static Universal Baseline Threshold of 14.

  • Grant Advantage: High ground, tactical surprise, or ideal tools.
  • Impose Disadvantage: Heavy strain, slick ice, blindness, or full cover.
  • Social & Skill Checks: Will vs 14 (Persuade/Deceive), Agility vs 14 (Stealth/Acrobatics), Might vs 14 (Brawn/Force).
WHEN DOES THE GUIDE ROLL A D20?
1. Initiative Target: Roll 1d20 (+ Tier Mod) at combat start.
2. Morale Checks: Roll 1d20 when enemy squad loses 50% numbers/leader.
3. Boss Escalation: Roll 1d20 at 50% Boss HP (1-10: 2 Minions, 11-20: Hazard).
4. Failing Forward: Roll 1d20 for narrative complication severity.

3. MOB ROLES (PLUG-AND-PLAY)

1. Brute: +2 Dmg (-1 Pace).
2. Skirmisher: Move pre/post attack.
3. Sniper: 8 Paces (+2 Dmg vs open).
4. Disruptor: Hit = Might check or Dazed.
5. Protector: Intercept adjacent dmg.
6. Commander: Aura (+1 Pace / no Rout).
7. Lurker: Ambush = Disadv on Evade.
8. Swarmer: 3 HP. Pairs force Disadv.

4. LEVEL SCALING & ENCOUNTER BUILDING

Scaling Brackets:

  • Lvl 1–2: Base Stats.
  • Lvl 3–5: Standards/Elites +5 HP / +1 Dmg (Boss +15 HP). Minion +1 Dmg.
  • Lvl 6–8: Standards/Elites +15 HP / +2 Dmg (Boss +30 HP). Minion +2 Dmg.

Encounter Templates (Baseline 4 Players):

  • Light Skirmish: 4 Minions, 2 Standards. (1 Minor Tag)
  • Tactical Clash: 3 Minions, 2 Standards, 1 Elite. (1 Major Tag)
  • Heavy Assault: 4 Standards, 2 Elites. (2 Major Tags)
  • Apex Boss Fight: 5 Minions, 1 Boss. (2 Major Tags + 1 Enhancer)
  • Party Scaling: +/- 1 Standard enemy per player above/below 4.

Modifiers & Morale:

  • Heavy: +5 HP (halve speed). Agile: Disadv on Evade (+2 speed, -2 HP). Lethal: +2 Dmg (-3 HP).
  • Dynamic Morale: At 50% squad loss, roll vs highest remaining Tier: Rout on 10+ (Minion), 13+ (Standard), 16+ (Elite).

5. TACTICAL CONDITIONS & REST

  • Conditions: Prone: Halve speed, Disadv. | Dazed: Disadv, halve speed. | Pinned: Might check vs 14 to move. | Stunned: Forfeit turn.
  • Rest: Breather: HP = Might x 2, RP = Will. | Rough Sleep: Half HP, full RP. | Sanctuary: Full HP/RP (Cost: 1 Minor Tag/player).
THE GUIDE'S GOLDEN RULE: FAILING FORWARD
On failed checks vs 14, introduce narrative complications (noise, tool break, 5 Flat Dmg). Roll 1d20 for severity (1-10 Minor / 11-20 Major).
